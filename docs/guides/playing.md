# Playing Ada the Navigator

## Campaign

Ada explores six story galaxies in this order. See the [version one rules](../../gameplay/rules.md)
for the full progression and the [player copy](../../gameplay/player-copy.md) for objectives and dialogue.

| Galaxy    | Chapter                                                                        |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Sunflower | Connect growers, a workshop and salvagers, then help start a community garden. |
| Whirlpool | Reunite a whale family, then help Ada understand her dog's need to explore.    |
| Needle    | Practise with Elder Loom and thread the Eye, then play at the school concert.  |
| Andromeda | Help Pip tell the settlers what happened and relight the dark beacon.          |
| Cartwheel | Support a miner facing intimidation, then help include a child at school.      |
| Phantom   | Free the blockaded settlements and confront Admiral Archie.                    |

Finish the galaxy's space quest and Earth resolution and recover its holodisk to unlock the
next galaxy. Ada can replay completed Earth chapters from her room. Continue on the title screen
restores your [saved progress](../../src/README.md#save-data).

## Flight

Ada has a rechargeable shield, independent move and aim controls, and contextual interactions.
The HUD shows her shield and Stardust. Laser has unlimited ammunition. Multi-Laser, Rapid Missile,
Heavy Missile, Scattershot and Phaser use separate ammunition, with the count shown by each
weapon. Ada switches to Laser when the equipped weapon runs out.

Stardust pays for weapons, upgrades, ammunition and paint at stations. Chapter completion unlocks
community projects in galaxies that have them. Confirmed part payments persist. See the
[economy](../../gameplay/economy.md) for shops, drops, weapon levels and project funding, and
[galaxy art](../../art/galaxies.md) for the visual reference.

The Paused card's Map shows discovered places and survey progress. Travel here checks danger,
tow and cargo. The galaxy map offers travel between galaxies and back to Earth. The
[flight rules](../../gameplay/rules.md#flight-and-combat) cover the travel conditions, and
[quests](../../gameplay/quests.md) covers the story objective and jobs. See the
[enemy rule interface](../../gameplay/enemies.md) for encounter and combat rules.

## Controls

The homepage [Controls tables](../../pages/index.html) show the live site copy.
The [phone runtime](../../src/README.md#phone) explains installation and the
landscape view.

### Flight

| Action              | Input                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Move                | `WASD` or arrow keys move relative to the screen without turning the nose. Hold `Shift` for low thrust.                     |
| Turn nose           | `H` turns left. `L` turns right.                                                                                            |
| Aim and fire        | Left-click to turn toward the cursor at the usual speed and request one shot. Hold to track and fire at the normal cadence. |
| Fire along the nose | `Space`                                                                                                                     |
| Use                 | Right-click, `E`, `F` or `Enter`                                                                                            |
| Fly on touch        | Move with the left stick. Aim and fire with the right stick. Tap Use to interact.                                           |

Each named control's icon follows its name.

### Menus

| Action                 | Input                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pause, go back or skip | `Escape` acts on the top surface and invokes Skip when visible and eligible. You can also click or tap Pause, Menu, Back or Close. |
| Move focus             | `WASD`, `HJKL` or arrow keys. `Tab` cycles focus. `Shift` + `Tab` cycles in reverse.                                               |
| Activate a button      | Click, tap, or focus and press `E`, `F`, `Enter` or `Space`.                                                                       |
