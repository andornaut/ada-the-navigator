## Scope

Redesign the points of interest and friendly objects in space from scratch, on the principles of the station and enemy ship redesigns: clean lines, simple large shapes, mostly flat colours, functional detail only, and forms that read as connected solids. It covers the friendly craft and characters' ships, beacons, buoys, landmarks, caches, pickups and other friendly objects, and excludes stations, outposts and enemy ships, which have their own items. Mock-ups in several styles come first; the user directs the changes, and variations follow once a reference is settled.

## Criteria

- Mock-ups cover every friendly object and point of interest at game scale on its galaxy's field, with Ada's ship for size.
- Production starts only after the user settles a reference.
