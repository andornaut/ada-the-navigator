## Scope

Replace the survey beacon's pedestal art with a horizontal satellite: two mirrored solar panels on a central transmitter. Covers both beacon frames, the job-art producer and the manifest entries.

## Criteria

- `beacon-a.svg` and `beacon-b.svg` draw two matching panels (lavender frame, three-column, four-row cell grid) mirrored across the transmitter, with no pedestal or ground foot.
- The transmitter is lavender with a white core and one pair of signal arcs, inner in frame A and outer in frame B.
- The 40 x 30 view box displays at 60 x 45 with the pivot at the transmitter centre.
- `art/jobs.md` lists the ids, display size, pivot and file hashes, and the producer regenerates both SVGs byte-for-byte.

## Decisions

- Symmetric panels replace the pedestal, so the beacon reads as an orbiting satellite rather than a ground object.
- The pivot moves to the transmitter centre so the world position matches the visual centre.

## Outcome

Both survey beacon frames use mirrored solar panels and a centered transmitter. The two signal arcs alternate at a displayed size of 60 by 45. The job art producer regenerates both frames.
