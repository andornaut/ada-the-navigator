## Scope

Each viewport resize in flight leaves canvas textures behind: live WebGL textures and canvases
created rise by about 7 per resize, and container memory by about 150 to 280 MB, until the tab is
killed at the browser check's 2 GB limit within 5 to 7 resizes. A player who rotates a phone hits
the same growth. Find what recreates them on resize and destroy or reuse what it replaces.

## Criteria

- Ten resizes between 1280x720 and 720x360 in flight keep live WebGL textures and canvases
  created flat within a small bound.
- A regression test covers the fix where practical, and the browser smoke probes the ten
  resizes.

## Decisions

- Keyline silhouettes are freed by what is drawn: every caller marks the silhouettes it draws,
  and each frame's sweep removes those nothing marked since the sweep before and nothing still
  draws, whichever caller made them, so new keylined kinds need no change.
- The remaining growth is garbage, not a leak: container memory still rises about 200 MiB a
  resize without a collection, almost all in the renderer process's native memory while the
  script heap stays near 100 MB, and a forced collection after each resize keeps it near 1.2 GB
  with no crash. Releasing replaced SVG raster images at once showed no measurable effect and was
  dropped. Cutting the garbage at its source, by re-rasterising only the SVGs in use, is
  [ada-0323](ada-0323.md).
- Some growth is retained even after a collection: about 60 MB a resize in the browser smoke,
  so the smoke asserts flat textures and canvases only and records container memory without and
  after a collection in `resize-memory.json`; [ada-0323](ada-0323.md) asserts it once its fix
  lands. The smoke's ten resizes after the silhouette fix:

  | Resize | Live WebGL textures | Canvases created | Container memory after a collection |
  | ------ | ------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------------------------- |
  | 1      | 767                 | 49               | 1331 MiB                            |
  | 2      | 767                 | 51               | 1407 MiB                            |
  | 4      | 766                 | 52               | 1563 MiB                            |
  | 6      | 767                 | 54               | 1638 MiB                            |
  | 8      | 767                 | 54               | 1804 MiB                            |
  | 10     | 767                 | 54               | 1882 MiB                            |

## Outcome

Keyline silhouettes no longer accumulate on resize. Resizes alternate between 1280x720 and
720x360 in flight, in the browser check's container (2 GiB limit):

| Build                                  | Container memory, steps 0 to 4           | Live WebGL textures | Canvases created | Result            |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| Before                                 | 947 MiB, 1.10, 1.35, 1.50, 1.65 GiB      | 478 to 571, rising  | 27 to 89, rising | Crashes at step 5 |
| Silhouette fix                         | 948 MiB, 1.13, 1.41, 1.56, 1.75 GiB      | 477, then 585 flat  | 26, then 37 flat | Crashes at step 5 |
| Silhouette fix, collection each resize | About 1.03 to 1.27 GB over eight resizes | Flat                | Flat             | No crash          |

Silhouette unit tests cover alternating re-rasters, frames no longer drawn and callers that draw
after the sweep; the browser smoke resizes a flight ten times, checks live textures and canvases
created stay flat, and records container memory.
