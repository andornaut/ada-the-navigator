## Scope

Review every art asset against the art principles settled in the station, enemy ship and friendly object redesigns, and improve or redesign any that look weak or break them: connected solids, clean lines and simple large shapes, flat tones under one light, functional detail at a medium level, no repeated parallel stripes, no thin rods or spars, no flat-plate forms, and nothing recognisable as a specific film design. Stations, enemy ships, friendly objects and Ada's ship are covered by their own items.

## Criteria

- An audit lists every asset group with its verdict and the rule it breaks, with rendered evidence.
- Each improved asset is shown before and after at game scale and enlarged.
