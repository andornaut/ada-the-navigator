## Scope

Give each station stable, galaxy-eligible stock independent of inventory, and replace confirmed ore
trades with immediate fixed-quantity exchanges.

## Criteria

- Two equipment, two weapon and up to two eligible ammunition offers.
- Unsupported ammunition is visible but needs an owned weapon.
- Exchange buys 2 and sells 1 in quantities 1, 10 and 100.

## Decisions

- Requests `station-stock` and `station-exchange`.
