## Scope

Use the reviewed six boss loadouts in campaign order. Preserve turret counts
and fixed front Lasers. Keep Scattershot for close defense with no more than
two mounts per heavy, and cap the number of weapon types at four per enemy.

## Criteria

- Named bosses have the reviewed counts, including three Rapid Missiles on
  Manta and two turret Lasers on the flagship.
- Unnamed capitals keep one existing specialist, use two Scattershot mounts
  and fill the remaining active mounts with Laser.
- The final swap gives Crossbow one Phaser, three Heavy Missiles and two
  Scattershots, and Broad Wing two Phasers, two Multi-Lasers and two
  Scattershots. The flagship's paired Charged Lances have separate warning
  windows. Each ship's Mine Layers share the existing four-mine cap.
- Preserve the existing route difficulty, hardpoints, saved clocks and
  equipment rules. Check how the later fights compare in flight.

## Outcome

Shipped in `2bfeee2d`. The final Crossbow and Broad Wing decision superseded
the earlier Crossbow lance pair. The checked loadout tests cover each boss,
fixed front Lasers, the whole-ship type limit, ordinary capital profiles and
the flagship's warning spacing.

The later matched flight replays compared all six encounters at the starter
loadout and with purchased Multi-Laser 1 and Shield 2. Phantom exhausted the
shield in both runs. Needle and Andromeda took less shield in those fixtures,
so the comparison leaves the overall difficulty curve open under Ada-0416.
