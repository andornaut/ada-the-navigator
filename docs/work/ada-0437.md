## Scope

Use the reviewed six boss loadouts in campaign order. Preserve turret counts
and fixed front Lasers. Keep Scattershot for close defense with no more than
two mounts per heavy, and cap the number of weapon types at four per enemy.

## Criteria

- Named bosses have the reviewed counts, including three Rapid Missiles on
  Manta and two turret Lasers on the flagship.
- Unnamed capitals keep one existing specialist, use two Scattershot mounts
  and fill the remaining active mounts with Laser.
- Crossbow and the flagship give paired Charged Lances separate warning
  windows. Both Mine Layers share the existing per-ship cap.
- Preserve the existing route difficulty, hardpoints, saved clocks and
  equipment rules. Check how the later fights compare in flight.
