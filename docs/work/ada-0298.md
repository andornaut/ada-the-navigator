## Scope

Produce `aboard`, `aboard-emergency` and the four holodisk overlays as SVG from the shared vector ship model, with their glows, haze and blur as SVG gradients and filters, in place of the PNGs. This takes `aboard.png` and `aboard-emergency.png` out of the WebP scope of ada-0285, which keeps the story scenes and `route-sky.png`.

## Criteria

- The SVGs match the current PNGs at display size at 1280x720 and 720x360 within antialiasing and blur differences, compared side by side, with any visible difference disclosed.
- Compressed bytes for the set drop from about 0.86 MB to about 0.15 MB or less.
- Load-time rasterization stays within Tech's measured budget on a mid-range phone.

## Decisions

- SVG over lossy WebP for the aboard set: the art is vector, so SVG is smaller and sharper on high-density screens.
- The rasterization budget is compositor raster at phone scale, 50 ms per SVG (game-coordinator,
  2026-11-18). Main-thread decode and draw under 4x CPU throttling is recorded for comparison
  only: the old PNG backdrops also exceed 50 ms on it.
- The blurred hull group stays a vector filter. Removing the blur saves 5 to 20 ms, and baking
  it as an embedded PNG is slower than the shipped SVG and makes each file 4 to 11 times larger.
- `image-rendering: pixelated` stays on the story art and overlays: Chromium rasterizes the SVG
  at its display size, so the pixelated and default renders are identical.

## Outcome

Delivered in c0e43938: `aboard`, `aboard-emergency` and the four holodisk overlays ship as SVG,
and the holodisk overlays still draw over the aboard backdrop with the backdrop showing at
their rim. Timings in ms in Chromium, medians of five cold runs, as compositor raster and as
main-thread decode and draw under 4x CPU throttling:

| SVG                    | View          | Compositor raster | Throttled draw | PNG it replaces |
| ---------------------- | ------------- | ----------------: | -------------: | --------------: |
| `aboard`               | 720x360 x2.75 |                 4 |            120 |             107 |
| `aboard-emergency`     | 720x360 x2.75 |                 4 |            134 |             107 |
| `aboard-holodisk-0..3` | 720x360 x2.75 |                 3 |       55 to 60 |        12 to 13 |
| `aboard`               | 1280x720      |                 8 |             56 |              83 |
| `aboard-emergency`     | 1280x720      |                 9 |             63 |              80 |
| `aboard-holodisk-0..3` | 1280x720      |           8 to 13 |       36 to 60 |        10 to 19 |

Every SVG is within the 50 ms compositor budget.

The storybook decodes the four holodisk frames and holds them before the holodisk plays (d0091a29),
so the frame swaps never wait on a first-show decode.
