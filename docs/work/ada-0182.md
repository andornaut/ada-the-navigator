## Scope

Move the shield to a bottom-centre pill, size it by Shield level, and draw its low state and recharge timer.

## Criteria

- The shield pill is centred at the bottom; its drawn bottom margin equals the resource pill's top margin, `HUD_EDGE + STATUS_PILL.y` (14.5).
- Pill padding is measured to drawn pixels (5, 3 and 8), so the pill is the bar plus 36 at each Shield level.
- Low and depleted shields use the burgundy panel `#532e35` with the red fill, icon and rim; the emptied track stays dark grey.
- The recharge timer is a thin track enclosed in the panel, filling over the fixed 30 s delay from the last hit.

## Decisions

- The user approved the darker burgundy panel and the enclosed recharge timer.
