## Scope

Draw the loaded but undrawn art the placement rules in gameplay@e7020417 put in play:

- the lit guide beacons (`beacon-guide-lit-a` and `-b`), every 2000 world units along the straight
  line between neighbouring Andromeda settlements, alternating a and b, with the dark beacon one of
  them until Ada and Pip relight it;
- the spare guide beacon (`beacon-guide-spare-a`), 96 world units beside the relit beacon on the
  side away from the chain, after Spare beacon is funded;
- the Needle channel lamps (`needle-lamp-a` and `-b`), one every 128 world units, 20 outside each
  edge of the practice channel, alternating a and b, after Channel lights is funded.

The project reader accepts the spare beacon overlay with no outpost and `channelLights` as a path
kind. Each draws over its light-field keyline. gameplay@e7020417 lands with the implementation.

## Criteria

- Each object appears where and when the rules place it, with its keyline on light fields.
- Proofs of the Andromeda chain, the spare beacon and the channel lamps at 1280x720 and 720x360
  with insets go to Art for review.

## Decisions

- New work is paused by the user (2026-11-19, via game-coordinator); this item waits for the
  pause to lift.
