## Scope

- Lay the shared Quests list's line buttons (the map's Quests and the Paused card's Quests,
  `src/ui/questsScreen.ts`) on the `art/map.md` "Quests modal" chip slot: a 28 slot from the
  face's inset, `markGap`, then the label on the 50 text column (`MAP_SCREEN.quests.textColumn`).
- Remove the local map's own Quests modal, which no caller reached, with its plain-line ring
  styles and tests; the map's Quests always opens the shared list.
- Browser smoke proofs at 1280x720 and at 720x360 with phone insets: the Quests list with a line
  whose target is unmapped, a detail page showing From and To with the unmapped To disabled, and
  a paged list showing Previous and Next.

## Criteria

- A line button's 24 disc is centred in the 28 slot, 18 to 42 from the face's left.
- Its label starts at 50; the visible disc-to-label gap stays 8.
- `createMapScreen` requires `onQuests`, and the map screen keeps no Quests card, pager or line
  styles. `MAP_SCREEN.quests` keeps only `chip`, `markGap` and `textColumn`.
- A unit test pins the text column; the browser smoke measures the rendered disc and label and
  captures the three Quests proofs.

## Decisions

- The disc keeps its 24 drawn size and gains 2 of side margin, so the slot, `markGap` and the
  text column all match the spec without redrawing the icon.
- The shared list is the spec source (Art, 2026-11-18); the plain-line rule is dropped.

## Outcome

Delivered in fbd6b019 and f8717e0f: the shared Quests list centres its disc in the 28 chip
slot, the local map Quests modal is removed, and the smoke captures the three Quests proofs.
