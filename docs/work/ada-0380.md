## Scope

Replace the separate rock, moving-rock and enemy placement passes with one pure,
seeded planner. Measure every spawn target against its requested count and gate
targets that cannot be met.

## Criteria

- `planRegion` takes rules, a seed and a region and returns placements plus a
  report. It has no Phaser or flight-state dependency and uses named seeded
  streams.
- Static rocks, then moving rocks chosen from placed rocks, then enemy group
  members share one spatial index. A spawn is accepted only when its footprint
  plus a fixed gap is clear, within a bounded number of tries, otherwise it is
  dropped.
- Stations and points of interest do not block spawns. Existing exclusion zones
  (arrival calm radius, protected areas, Needle channel) still apply.
- After spawning, moving rocks may overlap or hit other objects.
- The report gives requested, delivered and rejected counts by rule.
- Targets are densities per kind and area, owned by Gameplay.
- A target passes when at least 90% of sampled 3x3-chunk areas, over fixed seeds
  in every galaxy and distance band, deliver at least 90% of the requested
  count. The check runs in the default test suite within a few seconds.
- Flight instantiates planner output only. The old filtering and admission code
  and the duplicate cell logic in the pacing simulator are removed.

## Decisions

- Build the planner and acceptance check beside the current code first; switch
  flight only after measured targets pass.
- Includes the ada-0379 scope (moving asteroids chosen from placed rocks).
- Predictive avoidance of moving asteroids is separate work in ada-0381.
- Enemy groups are placed whole or not at all: retry other anchors within the
  bounded budget, then drop the group. Enemy targets count groups.
- Gameplay lowers resource and feature targets to measured achievable values and
  limits feature sizes to what fits between lanes. Lanes stay.
- Interim planner values: spawn gap 20, 24 tries per spawn, moving rocks chosen
  from slot and feature rocks. Gameplay documents or changes them with the
  target table.
- Merge the planner and measured known-failure table before targets pass; flight
  switches only after they pass.
- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers q-ada-the-navigator-gameplay-1): Place enemy groups before static rocks and keep the current enemy group targets; static rocks take the shortfall and are re-measured.
- D2 (user, 2026-11-25): Space group members and escorts by their hull radii plus
  the spawn gap.
- D3 (user, 2026-11-25): Enemy groups keep a minimum distance from each other,
  derived from the encounter spacing rule and checked across neighbouring chunks.
- D4 (user, 2026-11-25): A target is a density per open area; the request
  shrinks by the share covered by exclusion zones that apply to the kind.
- D5 (user, 2026-11-25): Group anchors sit on the encounter lattice with a
  bounded random offset inside each cell; the vacancy roll and kind selection
  stay.
- D6 (user, 2026-11-25): Admission counts only the group's own cell.

## Outcome

Flight uses one seeded planner for ambient enemy groups, static rocks, and
moving rocks chosen from those already placed. It reports requests,
placements, and drops by rule. The default suite checks the 90% delivery
rule across all six galaxies and all three bands with no expected failures.
The checked spawn-target suite took 4 seconds on `23b3bcee`, which shipped
the unified path and its authored targets.
