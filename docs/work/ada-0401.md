## Scope

Use a fixed top-centre slot for visual scan receipts.

## Criteria

- Align the notice with the top inset of the resource pill.
- Keep space for the top controls and move the notice below an active boss bar
  or crowded HUD row. Preserve Astra's foreground dialogue.
- Keep the two-second hold, half-second fade, and authoritative receipt
  contents. Camera motion leaves the notice in place.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-26; answers q-ada-the-navigator-tech-1): Fixed top-center slot (Recommended)

## Outcome

The local scan receipt stays in the top HUD row as the ship moves and shifts
below an active boss bar. Native tests verified both positions and the brief
receipt's two-second hold and half-second fade. The feature shipped in
`2d131d61`.
