## Scope

Every Shield level waits 20 seconds after damage and then restores 5% of its
maximum capacity per second when it can recharge.

## Criteria

- Keep the current Shield capacity, ore cost, free 10% floor, damage reset
  and emergency jump at zero.
- Preserve fractional ore credit through hits and Continue. Keep the HUD and
  details driven by the same timing.
- Check all three Shield levels and deploy the paired rules through normal
  delivery.
