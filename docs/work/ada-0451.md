## Scope

Every Shield level waits 20 seconds after damage and then restores 5% of its
maximum capacity per second when it can recharge.

## Criteria

- Keep the current Shield capacity, ore cost, free 10% floor, damage reset
  and emergency jump at zero.
- Preserve fractional ore credit through hits and Continue. Keep the HUD and
  details driven by the same timing.
- Check all three Shield levels and deploy the paired rules through normal
  delivery.

## Outcome

Version `392796ec` restored shields at 5% of maximum capacity per second
after the 20-second cooldown. Capacities remain 10, 15 and 20, with the
existing ore cost, free floor, hit reset and emergency jump. The tests
cover the full cooldown, refill and persistence of fractional ore credit.

Chromium continued at all three Shield levels during the cooldown,
showed the countdown, then hid it when recharge resumed. The public build
and worker manifest matched the checked artifacts byte for byte.
