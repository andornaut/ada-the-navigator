## Scope

Space buttons 8 apart stacked, 16 side by side and 24 beside a small button, and put every primary
button at its group's bottom right.

## Criteria

- Every button group uses the shared gap tokens.
- Buttons sit in a row when they fit, primary rightmost, and stack only when they do not, primary at
  the bottom.
- A named story action sits beside Previous whenever both fit.

## Decisions

- The gap is measured between button faces.
- Resume and Back to title are equal-sized side by side.
