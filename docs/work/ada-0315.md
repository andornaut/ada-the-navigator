## Scope

While a storybook page covers the game area and shows no galaxy backdrop, skip `refreshScene`, `draw` and `renderConversation` and stop Phaser rendering, so the canvas is not presented. `model.storyTick` and the save flush keep running, and everything resumes when the page closes. Aboard pages that show a galaxy backdrop keep drawing only the backdrop.

## Criteria

- At 4x CPU throttle on the holodisk page, the trace shows no WebGL canvas copy and `requestAnimationFrame` callbacks stay under about 2 ms.
- Story timing, saves and the return to flight are unchanged, and aboard pages still show their galaxy backdrop.

## Decisions

- `refreshScene` still runs under a covering page, since it shows the page itself and the
  navigation; only the flight HUD and the Pause card's local map are skipped there.
- The conversation layer renders once as a page covers it, so a finished emergency return's line
  clears as before, and is not laid out again under the page.

## Outcome

Under a covering page the scene skips `draw`, `renderConversation`, the flight HUD and the local
map, and the game loop runs Phaser's headless step, so the canvas is neither drawn nor presented;
ordinary ABOARD pages over the flight draw only the galaxy backdrop. At 4x CPU throttle on the
holodisk page the canvas copies per 3.6 s fell from 74 to 0, the `requestAnimationFrame` callback
median from 15 ms to about 1 ms (0.4 to 1.2 ms across runs, p90 under 1.8 ms, rare peaks to
3.8 ms), and frames land every 16.7 ms instead of 33 ms.
