## Scope

endpoint throughout the flash. This replaces the Ada beam's rotating visual
endpoint from ada-0307.

## Criteria

- The nose follows Ada's translation and heading. The visible endpoint and
  contact effect stay at their original world position, including across the
  world seam.
- Damage, target selection, fire-time reach, timing, and enemy weapon effects
  retain their existing behavior.

at firing and twice during the flash. The isolated model tests cover fixed
endpoint, spark position, unchanged hits and projection across the seam.
