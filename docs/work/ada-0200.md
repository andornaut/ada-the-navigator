## Scope

Resolve impacts at contact edges and draw them in seven shared families by firing weapon.

## Criteria

- Missiles share one explosion scaled to the blast radius; Laser and Multi-Laser share the compact flash; Phaser is the compact flash brighter and longer.
- Scattershot, charged lance, ring burst and mine have their own pellet, lance, ripple and shockwave families.
- Impacts use the actual world contact point and incoming vector; reduced motion keeps each family's silhouette and fade.

## Decisions

- The user approved the recommended seven families; ship destruction stays separate.
