## Scope

Give each galaxy one named capital boss. Use ordinary frigates and smaller
ships for ambient encounters. Adapt the existing capital hulls, hardpoints,
quest interactions and Phantom finale.

## Criteria

- Present one boss HUD with the ship name, captain name and hull health while
  engaged. Clear it on disengagement, restore the current state on re-entry
  and show Disabled when Ada can use the encounter's resolution interaction.
- Author an approach hail, a short line tied to a fight milestone and a
  resolution exchange. Later conversations can link the captains' orders.
  Keep the first five captains uncrewed drone commanders. Preserve the
  reveal of Archie's identity in Needle and his surrender in Phantom.
- Each encounter resolves once and preserves its state through Continue.
  Use the existing hardpoint, damage and input rules. Keep required routes
  winnable without optional bosses, projects or allies. Keep the canonical
  emergency-jump reset for Phantom's battle.
- Verify the real encounter, visible HUD, disengagement, return, progression
  and save behavior per galaxy. Verify no reward duplication and no surprise
  boss when ordinary encounters respawn.

## Decisions

| Galaxy    | Boss                               | Tie to the chapter                                                            | Progression                                                                                                                                 |
| --------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sunflower | Captain Tally, Hammer              | Outposts working together expose a gap in the patrol.                         | Optional. Reveal a repair supply cache and safer trade shortcut. The exchange circuit still earns the disk.                                 |
| Whirlpool | Captain Sounder, Manta Dreadnought | The captain tracks whale calls. Currents give Ada a way to flank the escorts. | After the reunion, disable the boss to clear access to the holodisk. The family still gives its location.                                   |
| Needle    | Captain Cadence, Crossbow          | Wait outside the Eye and use readable firing cycles.                          | Optional. Recover a transmission about Archie. Practice, the Eye and the main reveal work if Ada flies past.                                |
| Andromeda | Captain Redact, Broad Wing         | Repairing the beacon exposes fleet patrols using the darkness.                | Optional. The flight log reveals a hidden relay waypoint. Pip still owns the confession, repair and handover.                               |
| Cartwheel | Captain Claim, Outrigger Carrier   | Miners warn each other about a carrier claiming shared ore fields.            | Optional. Clear a rich ore field and earn salvage toward the disk price. The docking story still ends through miners supporting each other. |
| Phantom   | Admiral Archie, his flagship       | Preserve the blockades and fleet waves before the flagship attacks.           | Required. Disable and salvage the flagship for the last disk, then keep the map reveal and reconciliation.                                  |

Build the shared encounter and HUD with Sunflower first, then prove the
Whirlpool quest gate and Phantom adaptation before completing the other arcs.
