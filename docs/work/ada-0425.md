## Scope

Apply the selected numbered galaxy's existing fill color to its outline.

## Criteria

- The outline follows the selection and uses the fill color of the current disc.
- Preserve the marker sizes and hit targets, Earth artwork and keyboard focus cue.

## Outcome

The outline landed in `b7d6052a3`. Exact Chromium captures of that public
revision checked the first and second galaxy selections at 720 and 1280
pixels wide. The outline takes the selected disc's fill color. The change is
also included in public build `ec2ce101c`.
