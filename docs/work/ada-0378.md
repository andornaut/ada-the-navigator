## Scope

Let a moving asteroid collide with another asteroid. Both rocks ricochet
slightly, with each rock's size affecting its deflection.

## Criteria

- A moving asteroid can collide with either a stationary or a moving asteroid.
- Both rocks respond to the impact, with size-dependent deflection.

## Decisions

- Started 2026-11-25 at the user's request.
- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0378-struck-rock): A static rock struck by a moving rock drifts and settles to rest within about 3 seconds.
- D2 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0378-bounce): Rock-to-rock ricochets keep 0.3 of the closing speed, a soft knock.

## Outcome

Rock-to-rock ricochet follows the rule in
[Space encounters and feedback](../../gameplay/rules.md#space-encounters-and-feedback).
After each simulation slice moves debris, every moving rock that touches a
static, ore field or moving asteroid ricochets off it by their drawn outlines
at their current frames. The impulse along the contact normal keeps 0.3 of
the closing speed, each rock's mass is its drawn area, and the overlap splits
by mass, so neither rock ends the step inside the other. No rock leaves faster
than 80 world units per second. A planned moving rock keeps its new velocity at
constant speed, apart from its group. A struck static rock keeps its id, kind
and drops, slows evenly to rest over 3 seconds and stays there; protected areas
and arrival disks absorb it as they absorb moving rocks. River debris, quest
debris and legacy orbiting rocks do not ricochet.

Rocks are taken in id order and found through the chunk grid, so the result
follows from the flight state alone and a step costs each moving rock's
neighbours. The values are in `src/data/content.ts`
(`tuning.flight.rockRicochet`). Saves carry a settling rock's remaining settle
time with its velocity.

The combined gate covered two moving rocks, a moving rock striking a stationary
one, size-dependent deflection, and settling state through save and Continue.
Verified in deployed revision `68de36cd` on 2026-11-26.
