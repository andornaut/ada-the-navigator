## Scope

Document how the player jets render: the canonical materials on light and dark fields, and the logical-size rule that drops the core and keeps the middle band on small ships.

## Criteria

- `art/ship.md` states that each normal flame is three solid nested triangles, with the colour sources for explicit normal apertures, centered reverse profiles and legacy mouths on light and dark fields.
- At a maximum logical display dimension of 42 or less, only the `core` is omitted. The middle triangle takes the canonical main level's `outer.colors[0]` (blue, red or amber by engine level), with no root, scale or alpha change.
- 84 px frames and the enlarged title ship keep all three bands. The boundary uses logical size, independent of camera zoom and DPR.

## Decisions

- The boundary is logical display size so small ships keep a visible engine colour without changing the larger renders.
