## Scope

Remove the separate ore rock (resource rock) kind. Static rocks are ordinary
asteroids, and each rock's contents are decided by the existing size-weighted
yield roll when it is destroyed.

## Criteria

- No resource rock kind, classes or HP remain in placement, flight state, saves
  or art lookups; resource rock art is no longer referenced.
- The static rock target stays the ordinary rock target (29.84 per 3x3-chunk
  area); the ore rock count is dropped.
- Cartwheel ore fields remain authored clusters, made of ordinary rocks that
  always yield ore when destroyed; claim-jumper placement at ore fields stays.
- Destroyed rocks keep the size-weighted yield roll, including the exclusive ammo
  outcome.
- Gameplay updates rules, economy and player copy; Art retires the resource rock
  sprites.

## Decisions

- Keep about 30 static rocks per area.
- Ore fields become rich clusters of ordinary rocks.
- Rock contents are decided when the rock is destroyed.
- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0389-ore-field-yield): Ore field rocks skip the ammo roll and give at least 1 ore; extra ore and Stardust roll as usual.
- D2 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0389-ore-field-hp): Ore field rocks take ordinary asteroid HP (medium 5, large 10).

## Outcome

Static rocks use ordinary asteroid shapes and HP. Cartwheel's authored
ore-field asteroids keep their placement and guards, skip ammunition drops,
and yield at least one ore. Other eligible asteroids retain their ordinary
loot roll. Gameplay and Art retired the separate resource-rock records. The
later approved density increase raised the ordinary slot target from 29.84
to 44.76 per area. Both are live in `23b3bcee`.
