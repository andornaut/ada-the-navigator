## Scope

Specify the storybook page as a text and choice spread fitted to each page, and anchor each background by its subject band.

## Criteria

- The panel has a `TYPE.story` 18/26 text column beside a column of 36 story buttons, only as tall as its page's columns, from a fixed bottom edge.
- Each story background's manifest entry carries the canvas rows of its subject band; the background placement comes from the tallest page in its story run.
- Story buttons keep `SMALL_BUTTON_GAP` 24 between faces.
- A 220-character page with its heading and two wrapped choices stays under every screen's panel cap.

## Decisions

- The user chose story layout B; Gameplay set the 220-character page limit.
