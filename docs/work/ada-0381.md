## Scope

Enemy navigation treats moving asteroids as stationary at their current
position. Predict each moving asteroid's path over the steering horizon and
avoid it. Asteroids may still hit other asteroids.

## Criteria

- Navigation queries include each moving asteroid's predicted position over the
  steering horizon.
- Tests cover an enemy crossing a moving asteroid's path, with and without the
  prediction.

## Decisions

- Start after ada-0380.
- Sample, not sweep: each prediction pose carries its elapsed time, and the query tests a moving
  rock at its centre plus velocity times that time, so the existing 12 hull-against-outline
  samples measure relative motion without a new primitive or value.

## Outcome

A moving rock is a navigation candidate when its straight path over the existing look-ahead
passes within reach, and is avoided where its velocity puts it at each predicted pose. River
rocks are extrapolated along their current tangent. In the tests a frigate crossing a rock's path
is hit without prediction and clears it by the 80-unit berth with it; it passes a head-on rock
at the berth instead of 0.5 units; and it holds course past rocks leaving its path or receding.
