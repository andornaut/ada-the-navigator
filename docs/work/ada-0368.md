## Scope

Make each galaxy a 240,000-unit square with both axes wrapping. Transition
old chart saves once through the shipped format change. Set the expanded
landmark radius to 80,000 in the same transition.

## Criteria

- Use the exact world extent and verify periodic chunk ownership and
  deterministic placement when nominal chunk pitch does not divide the
  extent. Verify collisions at both seams.
- Keep the 12 retained landmarks, roughly 200 target, 3,000 spacing, 1,500
  clearance and 25,000 central survey boundary.
- Verify live entry, saved discoveries and Continue across both seams.
  New-format saves must remain valid across launches and old open tabs
  must respect the save fence.

## Decisions

- Use 240,000 units per axis instead of the earlier 280,000.
- Apply the one-time reset for old chart saves and change the landmark
  radius at the same boundary.
- D1 (user, 2026-11-23; answers ada-new-game-wrap-size): reset all saves

## Outcome

The 240,000-unit chart and one-time save transition reached production in
`199128c0`. The 80,000-unit landmark radius keeps the 12 retained landmarks,
200 total target, 3,000 spacing, 1,500 clearance and 25,000 central boundary.
The exact gate covered periodic ownership and collisions on both axes.

In Chromium, the deployed v14 build crossed each axis and restored the
wrapped position, discovery and visited pages through Continue. These cases
started from synthetic flight saves. A separate public v13-to-v14 update
backed up the old save once. Same-origin testing also confirmed that an
already-open v13 tab could no longer write over the new campaign.
