## Scope

Represent each newly emitted Ore or Stardust reward with five-unit bundles and
one-unit pickups for its remainder. Use one canonical glyph per resource.

## Criteria

- Keep reward events and resource kinds separate. A reward of 12 produces two
  five-unit bundles and two singles.
- Replace the four single Stardust pickups at a discovery cache with one
  five-unit bundle. Preserve other reward totals, probabilities, loot
  exclusivity and trigger guards.
- Preserve pickup identities and quantities already stored in saves.
- Use the matching resource glyph in world pickups and UI, with a distinct
  larger five-unit pickup. Keep existing currency UI sizing.
- Verify deterministic spacing using the painted envelopes of both pickup
  sizes. Check placement, collection, Continue and light and dark fields.
