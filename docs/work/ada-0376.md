## Scope

An emergency jump after shield depletion loses all undeposited Ore.

## Criteria

- Clear carried Ore on the committed emergency jump, including a single unit.
- Preserve deposited Ore, Stardust, equipment and voluntary return behavior.
- Save and Continue cannot restore Ore lost on the emergency jump.
- Show the loss line when Ore was carried. Keep the zero-Ore dialogue behavior.
- Verify zero, one, odd and even quantities across voluntary and emergency routes.

## Decisions

## Outcome

Shipped in `9ceeb969560292dfac739ad0a230c8c8dc536873`. Isolated model
checks covered 0, 1, 3 and 10 Ore on emergency jumps, voluntary Earth returns
and voluntary galaxy jumps, with Save and Continue. Deposited Ore, Stardust
and equipment persisted. The emergency dialogue named the loss only when
carried Ore was present.
