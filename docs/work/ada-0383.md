## Scope

Frigate Laser mounts fire at ship rate 1. The frigate's Heavy Missile keeps rate 2. Other archetypes keep their rates.

## Criteria

- The enemy fire rate supports a per-weapon override for an archetype, so the
  frigate Laser uses 1 while its Heavy Missile uses 2.
- Phantom level 3 frigate Laser interval is 0.57 s and Sunflower level 1 is
  1.20 s.
- Tests cover the frigate Laser and Heavy Missile intervals and one other
  archetype unchanged.
- Gameplay's `gameplay/ships.md` "Enemy weapons" patch lands with the change.

## Decisions

- Laser rate 1 and Heavy Missile rate 2 for frigates.

## Outcome

The frigate's Laser uses ship rate 1 and its Heavy Missile keeps rate 2.
The cadence tests verify the 0.57 s Phantom and 1.20 s Sunflower Laser
intervals, the Heavy Missile interval, and the unchanged capital rate.
The runtime and Gameplay rules shipped in `68de36cd`.
