## Scope

Raise the upgrade pickup chance for salvaging frigates, turret platforms and
capital ships to 20%. Pay 10–20 Stardust for frigates, platforms and hostile
stations, and 20–30 for capital ships.

## Criteria

- Use the same seeded inclusive roll in every galaxy. Salvage pays half rounded
  down. Preserve the scripted Phantom flagship reward and small enemy drops.
- Pay a hostile station's full roll once on its first disable or destruction.
  Continue preserves payout status. Site eligibility follows placement rules.
