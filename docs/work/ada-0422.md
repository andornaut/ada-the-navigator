## Scope

Keep the purchased row in place marked Owned on the current visit and
earlier revisits. After 300 seconds of active flight a later visit can
replace the stock slot with another eligible offer.

## Criteria

- Persist purchase and restock state through Continue and across galaxies.
- Keep time stationary while docked, paused or offline and preserve gear
  ownership, prices and service categories.
- Validate the immediate Owned state, duplicate-purchase rejection, early
  redock and eligible later rotation.

## Outcome

The stock and active-flight clock changes landed through `419a31c0d`.
The isolated 196-case regression run covered early redocking, later rotation,
and saved stock. Production Chromium showed the same purchased row changing
to Owned at 1280×575 and 720×360. The implementation is included in public
build `ec2ce101c`.
