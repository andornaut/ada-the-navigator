## Scope

Give feedback when the Practice Channel, the Eye or the guarded holodisk
receives a Use press without an available action.

## Criteria

- Show the current objective and refusal cue once for the blocked press.
- With every Eye gate lit, Use opens the collection panel and Continue
  awards the holodisk. Leave existing target priority and quest guards intact.
