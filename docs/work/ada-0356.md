## Scope

Fit Cheats without scrolling in two columns. Open it from Menu or Pause in the
browser and installed game, with one-layer return and focus restoration.

## Criteria

- Use Unlock all equipment, Unlock all galaxies, Unlock all weapons and
  Maximize weapon levels in that order in the left column.
- Put FPS counter, the equal-width +1,000 grants, Reset jobs, projects & quests
  and Reset save in that order in the right column. Put Stardust before Ore in
  both visual and keyboard order. Use canonical glyphs and full spoken labels.
- Remove Show tips again from Cheats. Keep the top-right X and centered Close.
- Keep existing action, progression and save guards. Keep selective reset
  separate from full Reset save and implement its approved Gameplay contract.
- Verify actual fit and keyboard order at supported normal and inset sizes.
  Verify Menu and Pause paths in both browser and installed contexts.
