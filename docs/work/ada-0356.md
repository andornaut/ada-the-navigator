## Scope

Fit Cheats without scrolling in two columns. Open it from Menu or Pause in the
browser and installed game, with one-layer return and focus restoration.

## Criteria

- Use Unlock all equipment, Unlock all galaxies, Unlock all weapons and
  Maximize weapon levels in that order in the left column.
- Put FPS counter, the equal-width +1,000 grants, Reset jobs, projects & quests
  and Reset save in that order in the right column. Put Stardust before Ore in
  both visual and keyboard order. Use canonical glyphs and full spoken labels.
- Remove Show tips again from Cheats. Keep the top-right X and centered Close.
- Keep existing action, progression and save guards. Keep selective reset
  separate from full Reset save and implement its approved Gameplay contract.
- Verify actual fit and keyboard order at supported normal and inset sizes.
  Verify Menu and Pause paths in both browser and installed contexts.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-22; answers ada-0356-selective-reset): Reset those activities and also rewind main-story quests
- D2 (user, 2026-11-22; answers ada-0356-rewind-galaxy-locks): Return to Sunflower and unlock later galaxies again through the story (Recommended)

## Outcome

The approved two-column Cheats layout shipped with browser Menu and Pause
entry, one-layer Back and focus restoration, named controls, and separate
selective rewind and Reset save. Art reviewed the installed and inset
layouts. The installed Pause check kept both reset rows available for an
existing save, restored focus to Cheats after Back, and left the save intact.
The footer uses Back because it returns to the invoking Menu or Pause.

The selective rewind in `0e62204d3` returns to Sunflower and restores the
story-dependent galaxy locks. Current participating tabs cannot overwrite
the rewind from a stale save snapshot. The runtime and styling shipped in
`0d96a53c`. The later FPS counter and tips decisions apply separately.
