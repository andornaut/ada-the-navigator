## Scope

Set the post-hit wait and the active empty-to-full refill duration to
25 seconds at each shield tier.

## Criteria

- Recharge at 4% of maximum shield per second with enough ore.
- Keep 1 ore per 1%, the free floor, fractional credit and depletion rules.
- Damage restarts the wait. Verify the live shield details and cooldown indicator.

## Decisions

## Outcome

Every tier recharges at 4% per second after a 25-second wait. Existing model
checks cover ore costs, depletion and damage. Chromium verified the Shield [3]
details and the actual cooldown strip disappearing at 25 seconds while the
shield stayed below maximum with zero ore. The change is live in `d4a7e407`.
