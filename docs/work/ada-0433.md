## Scope

Highlight the active story objective in the current galaxy by default. Allow a
player to track an active job from Quest Details.

## Criteria

- Return to the story objective when the tracked job ends or Ada leaves its
  galaxy. Advance the highlight with the current quest step.
- Use one existing marker entry, the wrapped bearing, the location icon, a quest
  badge and a short label. Move it from the edge to above the destination as the
  target comes into view.
- Mark explicitly revealed tracked locations across the galaxy. Keep ordinary
  marker range at 5,000 units and guide searches only to known areas.
- Pulse briefly on objective changes and render steadily with reduced motion.
- After Elder Loom's briefing, guide Ada to the green flag of the current
  Needle course, then to the next uncleared ring.

## Outcome

The live flight marker follows one tracked objective. The original label and
badge were replaced by the marker-only design in
[ada-0436](ada-0436.md). The quest log identifies the tracked quest.
Flight and marker checks cover story fallback after dropping a job, known
search areas, reduced motion, and advancing to each uncleared Needle ring.
Chromium verified activation and dismissal of the same marker in flight.
