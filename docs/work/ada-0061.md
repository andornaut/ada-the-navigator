## Scope

Rasterise SVG art at its on-screen device size, again on large resizes, and render at device pixels
with fractional camera zoom.

## Criteria

- Sprites stay sharp on high-density screens.
- Raster size is capped at min(devicePixelRatio, 2) for enemies.

## Decisions

- Rasters regenerate only on large resizes.
