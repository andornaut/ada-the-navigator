## Scope

Keep each quest's current objective, visible map name and contextual action in
agreement. Preserve quest order, reveal conditions and rewards.

## Criteria

- Check each galaxy's story handoffs in play and distinguish named destinations,
  pickups, fights and hand-ins.
- Explain Scan in surveys and mark the issuing station for a pending job lead.
- Guide the repeatable Needle run to the next uncleared ring and explain a fast
  miss.
- Show Pip's Accept action only when the courier route is ready.
- Explain zero Stardust in project details. Preserve partial contributions.
- Identify optional encounters in revealed boss details. Preserve normal travel
  permissions and story progression.

## Outcome

The published route shows the current objective beside the named place and
links a focused map destination to its quest details. Spoken approach cues use
the current quest stage. The Cartwheel station is named The Mining Hub in the
map and quest details. Its station type remains visible separately.

Browser runs exercised the Sunflower deliveries, Whirlpool reunion and guard
lead, Needle practice and Crossbow route, Andromeda pickup and hand-in,
Cartwheel Talk and Deposit, and Phantom flagship Salvage. The active survey
run used Scan, paid once and returned to its issuing station through the map.
Pending leads identify the issuer before acceptance.

Model and UI checks cover the repeatable Needle ring and fast-miss cue, Pip's
available and unavailable offers, zero Stardust and partial contributions, and
the optional encounter detail. They verify that the handoffs preserve travel
guards and story progression.
