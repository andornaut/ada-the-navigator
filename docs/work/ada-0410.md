## Scope

Show the equipped weapon's display name when the selection changes in flight,
including automatic fallback to Laser.

## Criteria

- Use the existing short notice style and hold for 2 seconds before fading
  over 500 ms. Center below the top icon row and boss bar. Dialogue paints
  in front at any overlap.
- Replace a previous weapon notice on a quick selection change. Preserve
  pickup, scan and ammo feedback.
- Emit no weapon notice for an unchanged or refused selection, opening weapon
  details, or restoring a save.

## Outcome

Deployed in `e0ef388e`. The notice uses the equipped weapon's name,
replaces older selections, and clears captain dialogue. Chromium verified
clearance and keyboard focus at 720×360 and 1280×720.
