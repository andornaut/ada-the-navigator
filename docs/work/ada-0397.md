## Scope

Every identified enemy marker is threat red, so a task-target heavy has no
distinct cue. Heavies get their own marker shape, also threat red, designed by
Art and wired by Tech.

## Criteria

- Frigates, capitals, the flagship, ore-field platforms and hostile stations
  draw Art's heavy marker in threat red.
- Small craft and retaliators keep the standard enemy marker.
- The marker reads on dark and light fields in every galaxy.

## Decisions

- Heavies get a distinct marker in threat red; small craft and retaliators keep
  the standard enemy marker (user, 2026-11-25).
- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers markers-flagship-cue): Frigates, capitals, the flagship, ore-field platforms and hostile stations get a distinct threat-red heavy marker designed by Art; small craft and retaliators keep the standard enemy marker.

## Outcome

Tech implemented Art's spec (`art/space-palette.md` "Edge markers") on the
`enemy-markers-red` branch, with focused tests and lint passing:

- Heavies (frigates, capitals, the flagship and ore-field platforms) keep the
  12 threat disc as their distinct marker, and a hostile station draws the same
  disc in place of its badge. A route guard adds the ink bar.
- Small craft and retaliators keep the 6 threat disc.
- Every identified enemy marker is threat red in every galaxy; a task colour
  never replaces it. An unidentified marker keeps a story or job task's colour
  and is otherwise generic.
- Every marker's edge is the field's foreground edge: white on Phantom, ink on
  light fields.

The `markers` capture set shows these markers on Phantom and Cartwheel. It
still needs a browser run and Art's review; the branch is not yet integrated,
deployed or accepted.
