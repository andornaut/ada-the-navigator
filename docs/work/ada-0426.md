## Scope

Profile the game when an installed phone app returns to the foreground and
when Ada approaches and flies through a Whirlpool current.

## Criteria

- Compare frame timing and work across uninterrupted flight, first visible
  frames after resume, and flight outside and inside a current.
- Fix reproduced spikes and compare the same traces after the change.
- Preserve game time, visibility and update behavior, current strength and
  appearance, and the default landscape play view.

## Outcome

The live navigation path rejects clearly separated enemy poses before
expanding their hull outlines. In matched desktop model replays, p95
fell from 44.66 to 42.45 ms for the Whirlpool case and from 12.65 to
10.97 ms for Crossbow. The paired runs produced identical model outcomes
and saves. These timings describe those fixtures and their test host.

A desktop browser resume witness preserved the flight clock and save
through a hidden interval. Physical Android resume and current timing
under the original conditions remain open.
