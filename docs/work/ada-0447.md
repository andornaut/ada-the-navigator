## Criteria

- In Storybook and standalone conversations, center Previous and Next together
  in the bottom row, 16 pixels apart, and center a lone navigation button.
- Next is the primary action on a linear page. Previous is secondary and
  follows the actual visited path. Forks show descriptive choices.
- Show Skip in the top right only when current and immediate next pages both
  offer Next. Preserve its destination, protected choices and Escape priority.
- Preserve optional-topic return, quest and read indicators, committing choices,
  saves and dismissal. Check first, linear, fork, before-fork and final pages.

## Outcome

Verified both dialogue surfaces in standard and narrow landscape. Navigation
follows the visited path, with Skip and optional-topic return using the same
choice rules. Shipped in `c0050a362`.
