## Scope

Ship one coordinated pass across all six galaxies. Keep the current global
hull, aim, fire-rate and spawn scaling as the baseline.

## Criteria

- Sunflower teaches a readable firing warning and exposed hardpoint in the
  optional Hammer encounter. Keep the required route forgiving.
- Whirlpool uses Manta's existing escorts in two distinct passes with a visible
  opening and positioning pressure from currents.
- Needle gives Crossbow alternating firing arcs and a visible safe lane. Keep
  the required gate course clear.
- Andromeda adds one warned two-sided courier ambush with a clear escape route.
- Cartwheel puts one patrol on a mining route among moving rocks. Allow a longer
  clear route and let pilots use existing physical rock cover.
- Phantom combines the earlier lessons in two blockade phases with a clear
  opportunity to disengage and recover between them.
- Preserve existing quest gates, save and re-entry behavior, and encounter
  budgets. Compare fight duration, damage and frame time with the current
  encounters before release.
