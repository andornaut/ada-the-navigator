## Scope

Wire `icon-fps-counter` into the Cheats FPS counter switch and fix five issues from Art's review
of the Cheats card: the Close inset, the focus ring clip, the scroll reserve, switch-row styles
outside Settings and a stale style comment.

## Criteria

- Close's lip sits 16 above the card's bottom edge, frame included, as Menu's Close and Settings'
  Back.
- A focused row's ring stays inside the row column's clip at the sides and the scroll ends.
- Every card scroll group (Cheats, the Quests list, install steps and the job list) reserves 14
  on its right while it scrolls, so Cheats rows are 346 wide then in every browser and 360
  otherwise.
- The switch over its On or Off state and the row icon alignment apply to every switch row: the
  icon's ink top meets the label's ink top, measured from the loaded font, in Cheats as in
  Settings.
- The Reset save confirm takes a click on Cancel over Cheats, with Cheats inert under it.
- Proofs at 1280x720 and 720x360 with insets of the scroll end and the Reset save confirm.

## Decisions

- A card with a scroll group uses the same end pad as every card, counting the frame, so the
  Cheats card, the Quests list and the install steps all end 16 from the card's bottom edge.
- The column's `FOCUS_RING.outset` padding holds the ring inside its clip, as the job list does.
- Art chose explicit padding for the scroll reserve, as the storybook uses: while a group
  scrolls, right padding makes up whatever the browser's scrollbar leaves of the 14
  (`SCROLL.gap + SCROLL.track`), so rows keep one width whether the scrollbar is drawn, thin or
  hidden.
- Switch rows share one ink measurement (`alignSwitchRowIcons`), which reads the label's line box
  wherever the row places it, so the centred Cheats row aligns as the top-aligned Settings rows
  do. `icon-fps-counter`'s ink starts 1 below its frame, as `icon-sound`'s.
- The Restart and Reset save confirm opens through the controls overlay's shared modal layer, as
  Cheats and Settings do, instead of a press view alone: the layer made every other overlay child
  inert while Cheats was on top, the confirm included, so its Cancel could not take a click.
- The FPS counter is a display toggle, not a `DeviceSetting`, so its icon is keyed in
  `src/ui/cheats.ts` rather than in `SETTING_ICONS`.

## Outcome

The Cheats FPS counter row shows `icon-fps-counter`. `src/ui/modal.ts` drops the scroll-group end
pad override, pads `.ada-cheat-items` by the focus ring outset and adds `reserveScrollbar`, which
`fitCard` applies to each card's scroll group and to the job board body. `src/ui/settings.ts`
shares the switch-row toggle and icon styles with every switch row, and `src/ui/switchRowInk.ts`
aligns every switch row's icon ink with its label's for Settings and Cheats. `src/ui/restartCard.ts`
opens the confirm on the shared modal layer. The browser smoke asserts
346 rows, the ring inset, Close's 16 and the FPS icon's ink alignment at the scroll end, and records the Reset save confirm, at
1280x720 and 720x360 with insets.
