## Criteria

- Draw a square two-pixel threat-red border inside the play viewport.
- Start at full opacity and fade to zero over 120 ms. A second hit restarts
  the fade immediately. Verify that it replaces a partially faded flash and
  that no pulse follows.
- Preserve reduced-motion behavior, shield feedback and camera shake. Check
  the rendered edge and letterbox at more than one viewport scale.
