## Scope

Size every modal by width family to game-policies product consistency: Large 688, Medium 500 and Small 400, inset by the 16 logical px screen margin, with full type sizes. Also pin the loading-failure Retry label colour.

## Criteria

- Every card names its family: Large (two-column Settings, Ship, Achievements, Quests, Map Quests, Shop, Jobs), Medium (Paused, Cheats, one-column Settings, install steps) or Small (Menu, Restart, Update offer, details, station drop, buy confirm). A card with no family is Medium.
- The card width is the family width capped at the available width, and its height is at most the game height minus 32.
- The Retry label takes the Start label ink `#181425` (7.27:1 on `#bfa06f`).
- Achievements, Map Quests and Jobs geometry is restated at 688 (634 with insets).

## Decisions

- Fixed family widths replace content-fitted widths, so cards keep their shape across screens.
- Settings is Large when its rows fit two columns and Medium when they take one.
