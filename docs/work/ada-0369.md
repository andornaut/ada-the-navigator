## Scope

Add optional scan, salvage, rescue, tow and lost-item opportunities near
regular story routes. Use existing interaction and discovery patterns.

## Criteria

- Aim for an opportunity every 60 to 90 seconds of fresh exploration
  progress near story routes, with fewer further out. Pace by exploration
  progress and preserve optional participation.
- Keep stable identities, saved discoveries, story, visit and scan credit,
  station jobs and quest-slot limits.
- Verify visible, usable encounters at normal flight entry and compatible
  Continue paths. Preserve approved reward behavior and world boundaries.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0369-cadence): Leads away from the near routes appear once per 150 seconds of piloted fresh travel, half the near-route rate, on the same counter.
- D2 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0369-optin): Finding a lead in space posts it as a job on its issuing station's board; Ada docks there to accept it into the single job slot.
- D3 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0369-issuer): A lead's issuer is the nearest station Ada has discovered when she finds it; no lead is offered until she has discovered one.
- D4 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0369-where): Farther leads appear anywhere in fresh space outside the near-route envelope.
- D5 (user, 2026-11-26; answers ada-0369-hunt-placement-fallback): For lost-item exploration Hunt leads, keep 1000 world units as a preference. Use the first seeded candidate meeting clearance. If none qualifies, use the first candidate with the greatest distance to its nearest heavy ship. Heavy proximity must never prevent posting a Hunt lead.
- D6 (user, 2026-11-27; answers q-ada-the-navigator-tech-2): Show a brief retry notice (Recommended)

## Outcome

Fresh piloted travel earns leads after 75 seconds near story routes and
150 seconds elsewhere. Scanning a job lead posts it at the nearest discovered
station with jobs. Ada accepts from that board using the existing optional
task slot. Seeded Hunt placement prefers heavy-ship clearance and still
posts when no candidate meets that preference. If Scan cannot place a safe
target, Astra gives a brief retry cue and Scan stays available.

The published runtime preserves site identities, board offers and scan credit
through Continue. Tests cover pacing, salvage, Hunt acceptance and payout,
and rescue posting and tow attachment. Chromium verified a rescue Scan and
offer persistence through Continue, and the Hunt issuer appeared on the Local
Map. A complete native rescue delivery and payout remains unverified.
