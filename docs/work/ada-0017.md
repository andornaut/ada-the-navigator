## Scope

Validate that Stardust pacing with the approved job rewards (the shared quest payout rule) meets the economy targets, using the pacing simulation.

## Criteria

- The pacing simulation runs with job rewards included.
- Its result is reviewed against the economy pacing targets, and any value change is approved before data changes.

## Decisions

- Pacing, kill-time and ore-income simulations stay deferred.
- The first run found a simulation defect: station targets timed out after 60 seconds. The corrected run put the job-assisted first weapon at 10.7 minutes against the approved target of about 17.
- Pay quest rewards at double the base instead of triple (user, 2026-11-18): `questReward(base) = ceil(2 * base / 25) * 25`.

## Outcome

With double pay, over 50 seeds of 90 minutes with jobs taken back to back, the median first weapon takes 14.0 minutes in Sunflower and 13.6 in Cartwheel, Cartwheel's holodisk 15.6, and all three Sunflower projects 21.8; jobs supply 84% of Sunflower and 73% of Cartwheel income. Runs without jobs are unchanged. `gameplay/economy.md` and `gameplay/quests.md` record the result.
