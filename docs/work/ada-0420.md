## Scope

Spend one Multi-Laser ammo per full level-dependent fan and set the shop
pack to 90 for 25 Stardust.

## Criteria

- Award 30 on world pickup, 270 for a new weapon, and 90 for a duplicate.
- With one ammo left, fire the full fan and switch to Laser afterward.
- Preserve existing ammo counts, rate, fan, enemy fire, drop odds and all
  other weapons.
