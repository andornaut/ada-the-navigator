## Scope

Apply one HP of damage to Ada and the enemy ship on a closing hull impact.

## Criteria

- Apply damage once per closing impact as the hulls bounce apart.
- Separate overlapping hulls without damage when they are already moving apart.
- Use Ada's standard shield and recharge rules and the enemy's standard hull
  damage and defeat rules. Preserve arrival protection and special hull floors.
