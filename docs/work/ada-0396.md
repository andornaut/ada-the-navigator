## Scope

Safe circles around stations, dialogue locations and arrival points make Ada
untargetable and remove enemy shots, but Ada's own shots pass out of them, so
she can attack a guard that cannot answer. Protection moves from places to
states.

## Criteria

- No spatial safe area changes targeting, enemy shots or damage.
- Ada is protected while docked or in a station menu, during dialogue, and for
  a short grace after arrival.
- Spawn exclusion zones around stations, salvage sites and arrival points stay.
- A guard attacked from anywhere engages and fires back.
- Tests cover each protected state, its end, and a guard returning fire near a
  station.

## Decisions

- Remove the safe circles; protect Ada only while docked or in a station menu,
  during dialogue, and for a short grace after arrival (user, 2026-11-25).
