## Scope

Safe circles around stations, dialogue locations and arrival points make Ada
untargetable and remove enemy shots, but Ada's own shots pass out of them, so
she can attack a guard that cannot answer. Protection moves from places to
states.

## Criteria

- No spatial safe area changes targeting, enemy shots or damage.
- Ada is protected while docked or in a station menu, during dialogue, and for
  a short grace after arrival.
- Spawn exclusion zones around stations, salvage sites and arrival points stay.
- A guard attacked from anywhere engages and fires back.
- Tests cover each protected state, its end, and a guard returning fire near a
  station.

## Decisions

- Remove the safe circles; protect Ada only while docked or in a station menu,
  during dialogue, and for a short grace after arrival (user, 2026-11-25).
- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0396-grace): Ada is protected for 3 seconds after a teleport, quantum jump or launch.
- D2 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0396-dock-danger): Danger blocks Dock as it blocks Travel; Ada must break contact before docking.
- D3 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0396-undock-grace): Undocking gives the 3-second grace like a launch; closing a dialogue does not.
- D4 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0396-needle): Needle's channels are normal combat; enemies still cannot enter them but can fire in, and debris bumps deal damage.

## Outcome

Protection follows the docked, conversation, and arrival-grace states.
Flight tests cover protected states, expiry, refusal to dock in danger,
guard fire beside a station, and enemy fire and debris damage in the Needle
channels. Spawn exclusions still govern placement. The feature shipped in
`68de36cd`.
