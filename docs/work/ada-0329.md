## Scope

Enemies start avoiding scenery from a longer look-ahead distance and evaluate fewer candidate
steering directions or prediction samples, so they steer earlier and more smoothly with less
per-frame work. Obstacle avoidance near scenery is about 60% of the flight game loop in
Cartwheel at 4x CPU throttle ([ada-0325](ada-0325.md)). The avoidance values are Gameplay's:
they live in `src/data` with names, and Gameplay documents the old and new values in
`gameplay/rules.md`.

## Criteria

- The longer distance and the smaller direction and sample counts are chosen by measurement,
  with the flight game loop near scenery at 4x throttle reported before and after, in Cartwheel
  at 1280x720 and 720x360 with insets.
- A fixed-seed simulation compares the old and new values: collisions with scenery, time stuck
  and attack-run frequency. Every regression is reported.
- The values are named in `src/data`, and the old and new values go to Gameplay.
- The full gate and the browser smoke pass on the exact revision.

## Decisions

## Outcome

Avoidance looks 25% farther ahead and checks up to five steering courses per clearance, using 12
prediction samples. At 4× CPU throttle in Cartwheel, the median game loop fell from 21.2 to
14.2 ms at 1280×720 and from 20.7 to 14.1 ms at 720×360 with insets. Each measurement used one
headless Chromium window. Four seeded 10-second fixtures recorded no scenery contacts, no stuck
time and two attack-run starts both before and after the change.
