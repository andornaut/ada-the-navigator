## Scope

Serve the UI fonts as full WOFF2 files and keep the TrueType originals as source.

## Criteria

- Liberation Sans Regular and Bold ship as `/art/ui/fonts/liberation/*.woff2`, served as `font/woff2`.
- Both fonts keep the full glyph set, outlines, hinting, metrics and kerning.
- The original TTF files are kept in `art/sources/fonts/liberation/`, and the OFL ships beside the WOFF2 files.

## Decisions

- Full fonts over subsets, so no player text can fall outside the glyph set.

## Outcome

Shipped on `main` in ba71a67c.
