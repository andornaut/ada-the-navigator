## Scope

Make shots, ships, the Phaser and sight meet rocks, ships, debris and pickups at their drawn
silhouettes, keep fly-through kinds passable with Ada drawn above, collect every pickup by one rule,
and damage the struck destructible obstacle on each admitted scenery impact.

## Criteria

- Every collision file Art lists carries the frames the game uses.
- Pickups collect within 20 world units of the drawn ship and the tractor beam pulls every kind.
- A traveling debris strike deals one scenery bump, not a hazard hit on top.

## Decisions

- Placement clearances use the silhouette radius; no two drawn rocks overlap.
- Request `obstacle-bump`.
