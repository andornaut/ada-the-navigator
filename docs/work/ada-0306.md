## Scope

Regenerate the art whose producers read the light galaxy fields set in `public/art/galaxies.json` on 2026-11-14: Cartwheel and Whirlpool debris (`cartwheel-props.ts`, `whirlpool.ts`), job nebula tints (`job-art.ts`) and the Whirlpool station, beacon and buoy (`whirlpool-station.ts`, `whirlpool.ts`). Whirlpool's station, beacon glass and buoy lamp take new colours that pass the producers' 5:1 contrast checks on the light field.

## Criteria

- Every producer that reads the galaxy fields reruns without failing its contrast check and byte-identical between runs.
- The regenerated art is compared with the shipped art at display size on its current field, and any loss of legibility is reported.

## Decisions

- Regenerate from the light fields rather than pin the shipped dark-field colours: debris and nebulae become paler.

## Outcome

The Cartwheel and Whirlpool debris, the job nebula tints and the Whirlpool station, Nursery beacon
and current buoy are regenerated from the light fields, and their producers' contrast checks pass
against the current fields with the ink keyline as the floor. On a light field the debris,
Nursery beacons and current buoys now draw over their 1 px keyline and the Whirlpool station over
its 2 px destination keyline.
