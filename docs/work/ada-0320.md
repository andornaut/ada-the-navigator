## Scope

`art/layout.md` describes the Cheats card as the approved Small card: one scrolling column of full-width rows, with Close as the bottom primary. This supersedes the Medium card with section headings. The FPS counter switch row gets a setting icon in the Settings icon set, drawn through its producer.

## Criteria

- The Cheats row in `art/layout.md` matches the rendered card at 1280x720 and 720x360 with insets.
- `icon-fps-counter` matches the other setting icons in size, stroke and alignment, and its row is reviewed in the rendered Cheats card.

## Outcome
