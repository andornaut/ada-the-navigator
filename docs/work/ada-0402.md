## Scope

Identify and fix the cause of flight slowdown when an in-flight dialogue appears.

## Criteria

- Capture comparable flight measurements with and without visible dialogue
  and confirm the cost attributable to the display path.
- Remove the avoidable cost while preserving dialogue content, timing, and
  interaction. Verify the resulting behavior in the same flight scenario.

## Decisions

## Outcome

Matched Chromium flight runs compared the same authored cue with a cold
portrait, a reused portrait and the overlay hidden. The cold portrait
required a synchronous GPU readback when its crop was first serialized.

Build `19e7b707` includes the CPU-backed crop. In two matched before/after
runs, crop serialization fell from 68.0 and 35.6 ms to 0.38 and 0.21 ms.
The frame intervals spanning serialization fell from 81.0 and 57.8 ms to
24.4 and 27.2 ms. Displayed pixels and save bytes matched. Overall time
to first portrait paint varied with PNG decoding. Installed-phone resume
and Whirlpool current timing remain under Ada-0426.
