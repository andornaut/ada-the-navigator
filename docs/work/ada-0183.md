## Scope

Generate each galaxy's layered backdrop, document the layers and caps, and set backdrop rock density and sizes.

## Criteria

- `art/tools/backdrop.ts` writes the shared layer rules to `backdrop.json` and each galaxy's sky to `<galaxy>/backdrop.json`.
- `art/backdrop.md` specifies the five layers, parallax, blend modes, contrast caps and reduced motion; the build fails when a layer passes its cap.
- Backdrop rocks fill 48% of 320 by 320 cells, with rare giant forms, every standalone rock larger than the ship.
- Needle's band is the fixed part of its second layer.

## Decisions

- The user chose backdrop style A, with backdrop rocks at parallax 0.8.
