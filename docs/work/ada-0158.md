## Scope

Put the HUD's Stardust, ore and carried item in one status pill beside pause, with the item notice and +N ticks under it.

## Criteria

- `STATUS_PILL` pads 6 at each end, sets each count 4 from its icon and each divider 6 from its neighbours.
- Each count is sized to its own width in tabular figures, at least one digit, with a snapping right edge; counts follow Gameplay's five-character rule.
- The +N ticks start 3 below the pill; the item notice is an opaque ink chip with its accent rim that lands on its destination.
- Stardust and ore counts always show, including zero, and pressing one opens its description.

## Decisions

- The user chose HUD option C, the single pill beside pause.
