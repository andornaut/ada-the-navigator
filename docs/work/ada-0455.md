## Criteria

- In active flight, WASD and the arrows move Ada relative to the screen.
  Movement alone leaves the nose heading fixed. H turns the nose left and L
  turns it right.
- Left-click within the flight viewport aims the nose toward the cursor and
  requests one shot, even across simulation ticks. Holding the button tracks
  the cursor and fires at the equipped weapon's normal cadence. Space fires
  along the nose.
- Right-click, Enter, E and F trigger contextual Use. Touch controls and
  shared menu navigation, including top-layer Escape handling, retain their
  current behavior. Menus and flight keep separate input ownership.
- Pair the rules, desktop tutorial, homepage and playing guide with the
  runtime mapping. Verify the flight and menu paths in a browser and confirm
  the production build through normal delivery.

## Outcome

Desktop flight uses screen-relative movement and independent aim.
Production Chromium exercised movement, nose turning, short and held
fire, Space, all four Use aliases and input isolation under modals. The
lesson, home page, playing guide and rules use the same mapping. The
checked source shipped in build `7d0bf2d8`.
