## Scope

Curve movement speed by stick deflection, draw Chibi's translucent joysticks with a speed line,
light ring markers near each side, and show and accept the sticks only on a coarse primary pointer.

## Criteria

- The knob stays inside the ring at full deflection.
- The speed line runs on the move stick only, to full speed's reach.
- Settings rows control stick visibility.

## Decisions

- Touch still works when the sticks are hidden, as the Settings note says.
