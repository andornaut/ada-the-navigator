## Scope

Offer Travel here for any visible named marker on the Local Map, including
charted points of interest. Show the Stardust icon and the 50 cost immediately
after the label without extra spacing. Align the galaxy name and mapping
progress card with the top-left page inset. Match its width and left edge to
the selected location card, with text left aligned in both cards.

## Criteria

- Preserve funds, danger, towing, and carrying guards. Unknown places are
  unavailable. Reaching a marker leaves its quest and interaction gates intact.
- Keep the galaxy name box clear of the top-right tools at narrow and wide sizes.
- Keep Travel here, its icon and its cost on one line. Move the entire action
  below the location details when there is insufficient room beside them.
- Preserve the native tap, hold and keyboard paths and validate at compact
  and wide play sizes.

## Outcome

Named marker travel and its quest-gate regressions landed in `5de92931d`
and `a1a6d79ad`. The matched cards and single-line price layout landed in
`e6e200aa2` and `6ecad86c0`. Production Chromium checks on `0ad0490c7`
covered narrow and wide map geometry and travel with the displayed cost.
The implementation is included in public build `ec2ce101c`.
