## Scope

Show Travel here with the Stardust icon and 50 cost on the selected place's
bottom-left detail panel.

## Criteria

- Keep the action available for discovered destinations and eligible stations.
- Keep the action visible and disabled for ineligible selections, with a
  concise explanation. Preserve funds, danger, towing, and carrying guards.
- Preserve the native tap, hold and keyboard paths and validate at compact
  and wide play sizes.
