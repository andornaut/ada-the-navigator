## Scope

Offer Travel here for any visible named marker on the Local Map, including
charted points of interest. Show the Stardust icon and the 50 cost immediately
after the label without extra spacing. Align the galaxy name and mapping
progress card with the top-left page inset.

## Criteria

- Preserve funds, danger, towing, and carrying guards. Unknown places are
  unavailable. Reaching a marker leaves its quest and interaction gates intact.
- Keep the galaxy name box clear of the top-right tools at narrow and wide sizes.
- Preserve the native tap, hold and keyboard paths and validate at compact
  and wide play sizes.
