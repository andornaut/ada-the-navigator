## Scope

On a resize that moves the raster scale, re-rasterise only the textures the current scene draws,
not every SVG loaded from every galaxy; rasterise the others lazily on their first draw.

## Criteria

- Container memory per resize stays flat both without and after a forced collection, and the
  browser smoke's ten-resize probe asserts it once the fix lands.
- No visible texture is stale or blurred after a resize.
