## Scope

Parameterize the ship's painted faces and export the purchasable paint schemes as recoloured idle frames.

## Criteria

- Cobalt, Seafoam, Graphite and Iris idle frames ship under `public/art/paint/` with `ship-paint.json` and manifest entries.
- Classic frames are unchanged.
- Every scheme but Classic is priced at 75 Stardust; Classic is free.

## Decisions

- Paint is a recolour of the same model, so every scheme shares geometry, jets and collision.

## Outcome

Shipped on `main` in 8041bd98 and c05b7896.
