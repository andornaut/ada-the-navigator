## Criteria

- Request 50% Small, 40% Medium, 7% Large and 3% Huge. Give Huge
  candidates room outside their ordinary quadrant when needed, while keeping
  the existing footprint, protected sites and passable routes clear.
- Use a bounded, seeded local placer that handles constrained footprints
  first. Keep results deterministic across chunk query order and seams and
  preserve the ordinary enemy allocation and authored formations.
- Demonstrate capacity at the existing ambient target and at comparison
  targets 25% and 50% higher. Record request and admission by size, rejection
  causes, candidate checks and generation cost. Live density uses the
  separately approved target.
- Draw one heading and one speed from 25 through 125 world units per second
  for each ordinary moving group. Cap each member independently at 125,
  93.75, 62.5 or 31.25 by its size, testing the path at that actual velocity.
- Apply the same size ceilings after ordinary ricochets. Preserve authored
  current and quest debris.
