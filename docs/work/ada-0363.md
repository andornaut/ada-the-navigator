## Scope

Switch to Laser or another eligible equipped weapon as soon as its control
is tapped. Remove the former one-second switch wait while retaining the
normal firing cooldown.

## Criteria

- Permit consecutive switches during sustained aim, movement and combat.
  Preserve current ownership, ammo eligibility, hold-for-details and modal
  guards.
- Ignore historical switch waits in continued saves. Switching must not
  accelerate firing. Eligible pickups still equip as before.
- Verify the actual in-flight HUD, touch activation and failure cases.

## Decisions

## Outcome

Shipped in `391789fa`. In Chromium, four consecutive touch selections
each took under 0.2 seconds of flight time while both sticks remained held.
The loaded fixture carried a historical switch wait. Holding a weapon opened
its details, preserved the selected weapon after release, and blocked a
covered Laser selection. Focused tests verified the normal firing cooldown.
