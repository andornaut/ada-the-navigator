## Scope

Settings opened over Pause or Menu draws its modal dim over the parent's, so two dims stack.
The parent's dim is hidden while Settings is open.

## Criteria

- With Settings open over Pause or Menu, exactly one modal dim is visible.
- Closing Settings shows the parent's dim again.
- Art reviews the rendered result.

## Decisions

- The parent stays drawn as the retained underlay (ada-0132) but its modal paints no dim, so the
  child's dim alone covers the view.

## Outcome

`src/ui/modal.ts` gives the retained underlay a transparent background, so Settings over Pause and
over the story Menu shows one 0.70 dim; the browser smoke asserts it at 1280x720.
