## Scope

Settings opened over Pause or Menu draws its modal dim over the parent's, so two dims stack.
The parent's dim is hidden while Settings is open.

## Criteria

- With Settings open over Pause or Menu, exactly one modal dim is visible.
- Closing Settings shows the parent's dim again.
- Art reviews the rendered result.
