## Scope

Show the shield and resource counts in HUD pills, sized to their rendered counts, with gain ticks
and item notices under the status pill.

## Criteria

- Counts fit at most five characters in a compact format.
- Gain ticks use the counts' format.
- Item notices fade centrally and report exact ammunition gains.

## Decisions

- The shield moved to its own bottom-centre pill (Gameplay `7e0db8e6`).
- Resource counters stay visible at zero.
