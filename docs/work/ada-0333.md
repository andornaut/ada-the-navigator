## Scope

Move the drawn touch-stick rings to 80 logical pixels from their respective
outer screen edges. Keep the bottom clearance at 50, the 8-pixel game-area
clamp, existing ring geometry and safe-inset policy, and Use's offset from Aim.

## Criteria

- The shared resolver places Move at (116, 273), Aim at (603, 273) and Use at
  (526, 173) in the 720 by 360 view.
- Drawing and hit testing use the same centres on base and wide views,
  including phone insets. The wide game-area clamp continues to apply.
- Update Art layout and Gameplay rules to match, verify actual touch input
  and appearance, and qualify the exact paired source through normal delivery.

## Decisions

## Outcome

The shared layout resolver now gives both outer rings an 80 logical pixel
inset, keeping the 50 pixel bottom spacing, 8 pixel game-area clamp and Use
anchor. The matching Art layout and Gameplay rules landed in `304c4f3fb` and
`08f2ae971`.

Headed Chromium captured the base, phone-inset and extra-wide clamped views.
Opening the game at 720 by 360 placed Move, Aim and Use at (116, 273),
(603, 273) and (526, 173). Hit tests matched both rendered stick centres in
all three views. A touch drag moved Ada, and a contextual Use tap docked at
the station. The wide clamp placed the rings 148 logical pixels from the
outer edges. The phone-inset capture measured approximately 80 on each side.

The normal 22:00 Toronto release `3c759d0b4` passed the exact gate and hosted
CI. Production reported that build after deployment at 22:26 Toronto.
