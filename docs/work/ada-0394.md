## Scope

Enemy hulls face their direction of travel but aim independently, so small craft
fire sideways or backwards out of the nose. Small craft fire only from the nose,
and large ships turn to their heading while turrets track.

## Criteria

- Small craft (swarm, fighter, interceptor) that are engaged and in range keep
  the nose on Ada and strafe, sliding sideways or backing off, and fire only
  while Ada is inside the nose fire arc, along the facing.
- Small craft that break off (hit-and-run retreat, fleeing, returning) turn to
  their direction of travel and hold fire until they face Ada again.
- Large ships (retaliators, frigates, capitals, stations) always face their
  direction of travel, turning around to leave; turrets track and fire in any
  direction.
- The fire arc and strafing speeds are authored data.
- Tests cover a crossing pass, a retreat, a strafe and a turret firing astern.

## Decisions

- Small craft face Ada and strafe, firing only within a narrow nose arc; they
  turn around without firing to break off (user, 2026-11-25).
- Large ships face their direction of travel and turrets track independently
  (user, 2026-11-25).
- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0394-arc): Small craft fire only while their aim point is within 20 degrees either side of the facing.
- D2 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0394-strafe): Small craft strafe sideways at up to 0.8 and back off at up to 0.6 of their class top speed.
- D3 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0394-passes): After closest approach, an interceptor's strafe pass counts as breaking off: it faces its travel, holds fire and keeps its speed.
- D4 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0394-retaliator): The retaliator's Rapid Missile launcher fires forward only, along its facing, which is its travel direction.
- D5 (user, 2026-11-25): Ships with fixed forward guns, the retaliator included, face Ada and strafe while engaged; only turret ships face their travel.

## Outcome

Implemented in Tech on `ada-0394-enemy-facing` with Gameplay's rules patches
(`gameplay/ships.md`, `gameplay/rules.md`) applied first.

- Authored data: `EnemyDefinition.guns` is `forward` (swarm, fighter,
  interceptor, retaliator) or `turrets` (frigate, capital, platform, hostile
  station). `ENEMY_FORWARD_GUNS` in `src/data/enemyNavigation.ts` holds the
  20-degree arc, 0.8 sideways and 0.6 backing-off shares; every forward-gun
  class turns its hull with its `aimTurnSeconds` (the retaliator's 0.35).
- Facing: a forward-gun ship engaged and seeing Ada within its engagement range
  turns its hull toward the aim point; its requested speed is capped by
  direction relative to the facing. Otherwise, and always for turret ships, the
  hull faces its velocity with `hullTurnSeconds`.
- Fire: forward hull weapons fire along the facing, only while the aim point is
  inside the arc. Retreats (morale and `hitAndRun`, every ship) and an
  interceptor's new `loop` tactic phase after closest approach hold fire.
  Hardpoint turrets track as before.
- A legacy saved ship's restored damage no longer reads as a fresh hit.
- Capture set `enemy-facing` (`tools/browser-captures-batch.mjs`) awaits the
  next capture batch.
- Retaliator pressure, shots at Ada per minute over 60 seconds, mean of 8
  seeded ships engaged at 300 world units: Ada still, 200 before and 199.1
  after; Ada orbiting a 150-unit circle at 120 units per second, 123.5 before
  and 111.25 after.
- Checked: focused tests (enemyAi, combat, ai, packRetention, frigateEscorts,
  flight, behaviours, shipPhysics, save, economy, enemy data, import check) and
  lint in the check container. Browser captures not yet run.

The production integration shipped in `68de36cd`. The crossing, retreat,
strafe and astern-fire tests cover the new facing and fire rules.
