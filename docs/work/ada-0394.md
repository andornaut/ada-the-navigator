## Scope

Enemy hulls face their direction of travel but aim independently, so small craft
fire sideways or backwards out of the nose. Small craft fire only from the nose,
and large ships turn to their heading while turrets track.

## Criteria

- Small craft (swarm, fighter, interceptor) that are engaged and in range keep
  the nose on Ada and strafe, sliding sideways or backing off, and fire only
  while Ada is inside the nose fire arc, along the facing.
- Small craft that break off (hit-and-run retreat, fleeing, returning) turn to
  their direction of travel and hold fire until they face Ada again.
- Large ships (retaliators, frigates, capitals, stations) always face their
  direction of travel, turning around to leave; turrets track and fire in any
  direction.
- The fire arc and strafing speeds are authored data.
- Tests cover a crossing pass, a retreat, a strafe and a turret firing astern.

## Decisions

- Small craft face Ada and strafe, firing only within a narrow nose arc; they
  turn around without firing to break off (user, 2026-11-25).
- Large ships face their direction of travel and turrets track independently
  (user, 2026-11-25).
