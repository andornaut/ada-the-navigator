## Scope

Large enemies are too easy. Heavies get more hit points and faster shots, and
moving heavies gain fixed front Lasers.

## Criteria

- Heavy part and hull HP double per Gameplay's table; hostile station hulls
  follow their formula; the flagship hull is 320 and every flagship part 18.
- Heavy projectiles fly at 0.8 of Ada's level 1 speed; forward-gun ships stay
  at 0.6.
- Frigates gain one fixed front Laser; capitals and the flagship gain two;
  platforms and hostile stations none. Each fires along the facing within the
  nose arc, is a weapon part with heavy hardpoint HP and counts toward
  disabling.
- Heavies keep facing their travel and never turn toward Ada to aim.
- The pacing sim's heavy kill times are reported per galaxy.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-26; answers heavy-tuning-levers): Double heavy part HP and heavy hull HP; heavy projectiles fly at 0.8 times Ada's level 1 speed instead of 0.6. Capital reach and heavy fire rate unchanged.
- D2 (user, 2026-11-26; answers heavy-front-which): Moving heavies (frigates, capitals and the flagship) get fixed front Lasers; platforms and hostile stations do not.
- D3 (user, 2026-11-26; answers heavy-front-count): Frigates get one fixed front Laser; capitals and the flagship get two.
- D4 (user, 2026-11-26; answers heavy-front-facing): Heavies keep facing their travel; they never turn toward Ada to aim the front Lasers.
- D5 (user, 2026-11-26; answers heavy-front-part): Each front Laser is a weapon part with heavy hardpoint HP and counts toward disabling.
- D6 (user, 2026-11-26; answers ada-0398-kill-times): Accept the doubled heavy HP and front Laser parts with their longer kill and disable times; tune after playtesting.

## Outcome

Tech implemented the criteria on the `ada-0398-heavy-tuning` branch, with
Gameplay's patches and Art's prow Laser commits, and focused tests and lint
passing:

- Heavy part and hull HP double, hostile station hulls are 144, 196 and 224,
  and the flagship hull is 320 with 18 HP parts.
- Heavy and hostile station projectiles fly at 0.8 of Ada's level 1 speed and
  the ring burst at 320; forward-gun ships stay at 0.6.
- Every frigate design carries Art's `prow` Laser and every capital design and
  the flagship `prow-left` and `prow-right`. Each fires the shared Laser from
  Art's drawn shot origin along the facing within the 20 degree nose arc, at the
  ship's Laser rate, and is a weapon part that counts toward disabling. Art's
  shipped hardpoints files carry the Lasers, and the catalog reads every
  design's ids and offsets from them; `tools/enemy-hardpoints.json` omits them.
- At the assumed loadout (Laser and earlier unlocks at levels 1, 1, 2, 2, 2
  and 3 by position) and the pacing sim's 0.5 hit rate, destroying a frigate
  takes 20 to 30 seconds of firing and a capital 40 to 59, longest in
  Cartwheel; disabling a capital takes 32 to 50 and the flagship 48.

The `heavy-front-lasers` capture set still needs a browser run and Art's
review. The branch is not yet integrated, deployed or accepted.
