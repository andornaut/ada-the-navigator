## Scope

Use smaller text for the quest detail's route panel. Keep Back clear of wrapped
route labels and long details.

## Criteria

- Use 14 logical px type with an 18 px line height in the route panel.
- Keep the other detail type, footer geometry, route navigation and focus behavior.
- Measure the phone layout and verify the full route is reachable.

## Decisions

## Outcome

The route now uses 14/18 logical px and long details scroll above a fixed
footer. Chromium verified no overlap in desktop and inset phone layouts,
including phone forced colors, and preserved focus after both Map input
paths. The complete gate passed at `dba2391f`. Normal deployment is pending.
