## Scope

Fire Scattershot in three rows for Ada and enemy capital mounts. Consume one
shell for Ada's entire volley and finish queued rows after the last shell
automatically switches her to Laser.

## Criteria

- Levels 1, 2 and 3 fire 7/3/1, 9/4/1 and 11/5/2 pellets at 0, 60 and 120 ms.
- Row widths are ±30°, ±15°, then straight at levels 1 and 2 or a symmetric
  ±4° pair at level 3.
- Preserve the existing interval, range, speed, fade and damage, including
  existing enemy modifiers.
- Details show the total per volley and describe the three rows.
