## Scope

Use a 200,000-unit square for each galaxy's navigable flight area and local
map. Keep periodic wrapping on both axes.

## Criteria

- Flight wraps at ±100,000 without changing velocity. Distances, object
  paging, map clamps and position projection agree with the new period.
- Preserve the 25,000-unit survey radius and the 80,000-unit landmark
  placement radius. Check required destinations and current encounter
  budgets on the smaller chart.
- Qualify fresh launches, Continue and save handling at the boundary.

## Outcome

`23fcead8` integration covers world wrapping, placement, map behavior and the
