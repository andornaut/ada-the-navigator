## Scope

Remove the manual Salvage interaction. Ordinary frigates, turret platforms,
capital ships and hostile stations award their defeat loot on first hull
destruction or when every weapon is disabled. Keep independently destructible
hardpoints and their existing behavior when a mount is lost.

| Defeated enemy                      | Upgrade pickup chance |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------: |
| Frigate or turret platform          |                   10% |
| Ordinary capital or hostile station |                   20% |

An upgrade pickup replaces the defeat's Stardust. Otherwise pay the full
approved class roll. Keep the current pickup contents and eligibility.

Allow hull destruction or full weapon disable to defeat the Phantom flagship.
The first eligible defeat grants its finale holodisk and exactly 50 Stardust
without another press. Preserve existing story and encounter conditions.
At existing exploration wrecks, Scan recovers the site's existing loot.

## Criteria

- Pay each eligible defeat or wreck once. Preserve paid and collected states
  across Continue without retroactive grants, including destruction after an
  already rewarded disable.
- Keep the finale holodisk, fixed Stardust and existing capital upgrade
  eligibility separate from the ordinary payout replacement.
- Preserve non-heavy rewards and existing scan and memory guards. Use existing
  feedback and contextual input, and remove stale Salvage prompts and artwork
  from the interaction. Wreck Scan does not open automatic POI dialogue.
- Qualify both combat defeat paths, wreck Scan, and already-paid Continue.
  Pair exact gameplay rules and copy, then follow normal delivery.
