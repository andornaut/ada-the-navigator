## Scope

Use only hull HP for combat damage and destruction. Visible weapon mounts
keep their weapons and muzzle positions, and can fire until the enemy is
destroyed. Remove independent damage to weapon, engine and command parts,
the weapon-disable defeat path, and the manual Salvage action.

For ordinary heavies, the first hull destruction rolls for an upgrade pickup.
An upgrade replaces the full class Stardust roll. Keep the existing pickup
contents and eligibility.

| Destroyed enemy                     | Upgrade pickup chance |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------: |
| Frigate or turret platform          |                   10% |
| Ordinary capital or hostile station |                   20% |

After the three waves and existing story gates, the active Phantom flagship
surrenders when damage would bring it to 16 of its 320 hull HP or lower.
The hit clamps at 16 even if it would be lethal. The flagship stops attacking,
counts as noncombat and remains damage-immune. Its large HUD says Surrendered.
Play the approved Ada and Archie lines as an in-flight overlay, award the
holodisk and exactly 50 Stardust once, and run the existing aftermath.
At existing exploration wrecks, Scan recovers that site's existing loot.

## Criteria

- Preserve completed progress and already paid or claimed states across
  Continue. Cover legacy part damage and disabled unclaimed encounters
  without duplicate payouts or softlocks.
- Keep the finale holodisk and fixed Stardust separate from ordinary payout
  replacement. Preserve non-heavy rewards and existing scan and memory
  guards, and show brief feedback without automatic POI dialogue.
- Area weapons deal one normal hull hit per enemy per event. Keep loadouts,
  aim, timing, hull shapes and muzzle locations. Remove obsolete hardpoint
  meters, target routing, disabled and Salvage prompts.
- Visible engaged ordinary frigates, turret platforms, capitals and hostile
  stations get compact hull bars. Named bosses use their existing large HUD
  hull meter only. Keep boss labels and engagement behavior.
- Qualify hull damage, defeat, wreck Scan and Continue. Pair exact rules and
  copy, then follow normal delivery.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-28): Use hull HP only and keep all mounts active until
  destruction, including the flagship. Replaces independent parts and
  full-weapon-disable defeat.
- D2 (user, 2026-11-28): The active flagship surrenders at 5% hull instead
  of being destroyed. The same threshold applies to every damage path,
  including ramming. Preserve collision motion and Ada's contact damage,
  and qualify a second hit in the same step and after Continue.
