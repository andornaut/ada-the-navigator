## Scope

Merge Shield recharge into the Shield upgrade, set its levels, start recharge 30 s after the last
damage, and show the delay as a strip along the bar's bottom edge.

## Criteria

- The bar length follows the Shield level.
- The strip fills over the delay and hides once it elapses.
- Every shield bar shares one background.

## Decisions

- The recharge pulse around Ada's ship was removed.
- Shield recharge spends ore proportionally, with paid ticks.
