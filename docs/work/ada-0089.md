## Scope

Expand the quest panel with mapped endpoints and read-only details, build details from canonical
progress, page them within the card and open a carried quest directly from its HUD resource or
contextual Use.

## Criteria

- Quest destinations are distinct from current map targets.
- Dismissal returns to the quest's origin.
- Requirements reflect actual delivery and cargo state.

## Decisions

- The detail list action reads All quests.
