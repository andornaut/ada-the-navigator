## Scope

Double the existing final rounded payout for station jobs and repeatable
activities through the common reward calculation.

## Criteria

- Use the same amount for display and credit exactly once.
- Preserve base saved rewards, already paid balances and completion guards.
- Keep prices, projects, loot, story progression and galaxy density.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-23; answers ada-story-project-stardust): Keep payouts to optional quests

## Outcome

The new payouts reached production in `ffeb864f`. Station jobs and
repeatable optional activities display and award twice the prior rounded
Stardust payout. Story payouts, prices, projects, loot and galaxy density
keep their existing rules.

The checked payout tests cover older saved offers and held activities,
matching display and credited amounts, and repeated completion. The saved
base rewards and prior balances are preserved. A completed activity cannot
award Stardust again.
