## Scope

Use consistent story quest, job and community project colours in category
headers and rows, on the map, and on offscreen target markers. Keep the
existing target registry and identification rules.

## Criteria

- Eligible unknown targets use a question mark. Its tint conveys the task
  category or the generic unknown state without revealing the target's name or
  kind. The actual glyph appears after existing identification.
- Cover eligible story, job and project targets alongside ships, stations and
  other points of interest. Keep existing range, visibility, window and
  knowledge limits.
- Draw one marker per physical target even when several tasks share a site.
  Reconcile category overlap with Gameplay and use Art's checked palette.
- Verify category consistency and the unknown-to-known transition rules in
  actual list, map and offscreen views, including overlapping task sites and
  out-of-range targets. The checked source follows normal delivery.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-22; answers ada-0341-shared-site-tint): Story quest first, then job, then community project (Recommended)

## Outcome

The shared task colors reached production in `fdd76220` on 2026-11-22.
In Chromium the sensed story target used a teal question mark without
exposing its identity. Flying toward the rendered target revealed its
glyph without changing ordinary chart knowledge. An overlapping story
and job drew one teal marker and one Map chip. A distant target kept
its known Map chip and lost its edge marker outside the flight range.

The approved project color is used in project lists. The current
Gameplay contract has no active project navigation target, so an
offer, donation or decorative project asset does not add a marker.
