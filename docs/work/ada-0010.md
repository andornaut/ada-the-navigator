## Scope

Place the touch sticks and the shield pill to the Gameplay rules, and specify the hit flash Art draws when Ada takes damage.

## Criteria

- Each stick's drawn ring edge sits 50 logical px from its outer screen side and 50 above the screen bottom, ignoring safe insets, moved inward only to stay at least `STICK_AREA_MARGIN` (8) inside the game area.
- The shield pill is centred on the HUD rect with its drawn bottom 14.5 above the viewport bottom.
- `art/layout.md` tabulates ring, rest centre, shield and Use positions at 720x360 and 844x390, with and without phone insets.
- The hit flash uses the threat red from `art/space-palette.md`, fades out, and stays fixed on screen while the camera shakes.

## Decisions

- Threat red with a fade out, so the flash matches the existing danger colour and does not compete with the HUD.
- The flash holds still during the shake so it reads as a screen state, not as a moving object.

## Outcome

Specified on `art-sticks-hit-flash` (cc2c2170).
