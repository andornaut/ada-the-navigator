## Scope

Charge 50 Stardust for a successful local map teleport. Show the price with
its Stardust glyph in the existing Travel here control.

## Criteria

- Require the full balance before travel and charge the price once after a
  successful local jump. Leave position and balance unchanged on refusal.
- Preserve existing destination, danger, tow and carry eligibility, action
  routing, focus and label. Preserve travel outside the local map.
- Verify the actual map-to-model path and persisted balance in the normal gate.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-26): Local map travel costs 50 Stardust on success.

## Outcome

Deployed in `7b8c1f1a`. The Local Map shows a 50 Stardust price and
requires that balance. A successful jump charges once. The checked model cases
cover insufficient funds, successful travel and the reloaded balance.
