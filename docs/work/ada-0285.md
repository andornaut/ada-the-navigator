## Scope

Encode the 20 `public/art/story-composed/` scenes as lossy WebP quality 80 and the three root PNGs (`aboard.png`, `aboard-emergency.png`, `route-sky.png`) as lossy WebP quality 90, with the producers and manifests updated.

The scope is narrowed: the 20 story scenes stay in it at quality 80 and `route-sky.png` at
quality 90, while `aboard.png` and `aboard-emergency.png` moved to the aboard SVG item, which
ships them as SVG. This narrows the scope and does not supersede the item.

## Criteria

- Each encoded image is compared with its source at display size, and any visible softening is disclosed.
- Compressed bytes drop by about 1.2 MB; before and after sizes are reported per file.
- Every reference resolves the new files and no old file ships.
