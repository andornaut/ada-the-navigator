## Scope

Encode the 20 `public/art/story-composed/` scenes as lossy WebP quality 80 and the three root PNGs (`aboard.png`, `aboard-emergency.png`, `route-sky.png`) as lossy WebP quality 90, with the producers and manifests updated.

The scope is narrowed: the 20 story scenes stay in it at quality 80 and `route-sky.png` at
quality 90, while `aboard.png` and `aboard-emergency.png` moved to [ada-0298](ada-0298.md), which
ships them as SVG. This narrows the scope and does not supersede the item.

## Criteria

- Each encoded image is compared with its source at display size, and any visible softening is disclosed.
- Compressed bytes drop by about 1.2 MB; before and after sizes are reported per file.
- Every reference resolves the new files and no old file ships.

## Decisions

## Outcome

The 20 story scenes ship as lossy WebP quality 80, 1,967,728 to 219,204 bytes. A decoded check
per scene at display size (mean at most 1.0, p99 at most 6, max at most 48) fails the build; the
worst scene measures 0.813, 4 and 22, and the exact rebuild check on the lossless pieces is
unchanged. `route-sky.webp` ships as lossy WebP quality 90, 332,991 to 30,100 bytes, and its
locked-dash luminance guard holds on the decoded image (peak 0.02824 against 0.02856, contrast
6.60:1). The producers decode through art-kit 0.32.0 `webp.decodeWebp` (ada-0327).
