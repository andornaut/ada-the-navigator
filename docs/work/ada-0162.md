## Scope

Retint each galaxy's field, make Sunflower a light farm-yellow field, and rule the keylines and edges that keep play objects readable on it.

## Criteria

- `galaxies.json` retints the background and environment roles to one Oklab hue per galaxy; the build fails if a space signal drops below 5:1.
- Sunflower's field is `#D8C994`, with a light-field rule for keylines, shot and beam ink edges, indicator strokes and darker jets.
- The keyline dilates the silhouette thresholded at alpha 0.5 with a square kernel; blasts ink-edge only the ring stroke and flash outline.
- Every HUD panel and the shield track draw ink at 0.65 in every galaxy.

## Decisions

- The user chose Sunflower option A and panels at 0.65 everywhere.

## Outcome

Shipped on `main` in ac58bbc2, 85ecd9e9, 19debd88, a9176aa1, c0ec6dac and 3f9dc21f.
