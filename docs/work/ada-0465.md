## Scope

Apply the approved hull HP progression in Sunflower, Whirlpool, Needle,
Andromeda, Cartwheel and Phantom. Roll whole-number Stardust rewards by
archetype independently of galaxy.

| Enemy                     | Hull HP in route order                | Stardust              |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Swarm                     | 2, 2, 4, 4, 6, 6                      | 1–3                   |
| Fighter, including escort | 4, 4, 7, 7, 10, 10                    | 2–5                   |
| Interceptor               | 3, 3, 5, 5, 8, 8                      | 3–5                   |
| Retaliator                | 15, 15, 20, 20, 25, 25                | 4–6                   |
| Frigate                   | 40, 45, 50, 55, 60, 65                | 10–20                 |
| Capital                   | 80, 100, 120, 140, 160, 180           | 20–30                 |
| Turret platform           | 60, 65, 70, 75, 80, 85                | 15–25                 |
| Hostile station           | Absent, absent, absent, 175, 200, 225 | 20–30                 |
| Phantom flagship          | 320                                   | 50 on special salvage |

## Criteria

- Use the existing route position and retain the 1 HP floor. Preserve hardpoint
  HP and counts, progression and save policy.
- Pay ordinary heavy salvage half the rolled amount rounded down. Pay hostile
  stations the full amount once on first disable or destruction. Preserve ammo
  and all other drops.
- Pair the exact runtime and focused endpoint and Continue checks with the
  ships and economy rules. Follow normal delivery.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-28): Use 60, 65, 70, 75, 80 and 85 hull HP for turret
  platforms in route order and roll 15–25 Stardust in every galaxy.
  Replaces the previous platform row. Keep existing placement and mounts.
