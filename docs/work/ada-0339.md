## Scope

Open the station on one keeper paragraph and portrait. Use shared left-aligned
Stardust and Ore balances and a persistent footer with Shop, Jobs & Projects,
and Exchange on the left and primary Undock on the right.

## Criteria

- The ordinary keeper greeting has no Next or Skip. Show services immediately.
  Preserve mandatory choices and guarded arrival behavior.
- Shop shows two fixed side-by-side groups selected per station from Weapons,
  Equipment and Ammo. Each pane shows up to four offers, with compact headings
  and no inner tabs, scrolling or pagination. Preserve per-station variation,
  eligibility, prices and purchase restrictions.
- Every purchase shows a compact one-icon confirmation with price, Cancel
  and Buy. Omit the projected player balance. Preserve purchase guards and
  modal isolation. Return to the same offer or nearest available offer
  with focus restored.
- Jobs & Projects is the footer label and page title. Use the same compact
  two-pane layout as Shop, with Jobs on the left and Community projects on
  the right. Fit up to four offers on one screen without a pager. Replenish
  completed jobs through the existing board.
- Selecting a job or project row opens a detail dialog with Cancel and the
  action for that offer. Job details show Reward, the amount and the Stardust
  icon, with the equivalent spoken text. Keep ore in sentences as text.
  Project details show the contribution and any amount still needed. Omit
  the projected player balance. Keep action buttons out of the list rows.
  Preserve held jobs, donation guards and focus return. Open the page at
  project-only stations. Keep the Map Quests button and the Story quests
  heading.
- Exchange has Buy Stardust and Buy ore rows, each with immediate 1, 10 and
  100 actions and a received-resource icon on every button. Show the rate
  below each buy label using the canonical unit cost, both resource icons and
  equivalent spoken text. Place the quantity buttons beside the rates.
  Align the quantity columns across both rows. Keep equal visible gaps
  before the first quantity, between quantities and after the last face.
  Unavailable quantities have a flat muted face lighter than the row, with
  readable labels and icons. Update both header balances atomically after a
  trade. Preserve keyboard access and brief refusal feedback. Omit the
  success summary, free-form input and confirmation.
- Check the live station at base and phone-inset sizes. Include full and sparse
  stock, a full Jobs & Projects page, detail dialogs and focus return, and
  successful and refused trades in both directions. The checked source follows
  normal delivery.

## Decisions

- The final mock governs layout and Exchange pricing. Its sample offers,
  prices and keeper copy do not set the station-specific Gameplay values.
- D1 (user, 2026-11-22; answers ada-0339-25-75): Enlarge world station and POI art by 25–75%. Jobs & Projects keeps the equal Shop-style panes.
