## Scope

Open the station on one keeper paragraph and portrait. Use shared left-aligned
Stardust and Ore balances and a persistent footer with Shop, Jobs & Projects,
and Exchange on the left and primary Undock on the right.

## Criteria

- The ordinary keeper greeting has no Next or Skip. Show services immediately.
  Preserve mandatory choices and guarded arrival behavior.
- Shop shows two fixed side-by-side groups selected per station from Weapons,
  Equipment and Ammo. Each pane shows up to four offers, with compact headings
  and no inner tabs, scrolling or pagination. Preserve per-station variation,
  eligibility, prices and purchase restrictions.
- Every purchase shows a compact one-icon confirmation with price, remaining
  balance, Cancel and Buy. Preserve purchase guards and modal isolation. Return
  to the same offer or nearest available offer with focus restored.
- Jobs & Projects is the footer label and page title. Match the Shop split,
  with Jobs on the left and Community projects on the right. Preserve held
  jobs, project details and donations. Open the page at project-only stations.
  Keep the Map Quests button and the Story quests heading.
- Exchange has Buy Stardust and Buy ore rows, each with immediate 1, 10 and
  100 actions and a received-resource icon on every button. Each received unit
  costs two units of the other resource in either direction. Update both balances
  atomically, show feedback for trades and refusals, and leave out free-form
  input and confirmation.
- Check the live station at base and phone-inset sizes, including full and
  sparse stock, confirmation and return, projects, and successful and refused
  trades in both directions. The checked source follows normal delivery.

## Decisions

- The final mock governs layout and Exchange pricing. Its sample offers,
  prices and keeper copy do not set the station-specific Gameplay values.
