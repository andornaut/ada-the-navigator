## Scope

Move the FPS counter toggle from Cheats to the top of the right column in
Settings. Keep the readout in its original bottom-right position.

## Criteria

- Align the toggle inline with the settings in its column.
- Keep the readout's existing parent, rotation, safe-area inset and layering.
- Keep the existing device preference and Off default. Preserve save and
  cheat-session behavior in browser and installed modes.
- Keep Show tips again available in Cheats.

## Decisions

- The later right-column setting decision supersedes the earlier proposal
  to relocate the readout.

## Outcome

The FPS counter is first in the right Settings column and starts Off each
session. Chromium verified the actual desktop and installed-inset layouts, the
original bottom-right readout, keyboard and touch, and no preference or save
writes. Show tips again stays in Cheats. The combined gate passed at
`03feadc2`, and `d4a7e407` went live on 2026-11-23 UTC.
