## Scope

Round coordinates in every Art producer's generated SVG to one decimal, unless a set proves it needs more, and minify the output.

## Criteria

- Generated SVG coordinates carry at most one decimal; any set kept at higher precision is named with its reason.
- Rendered frames differ from the current ones by no more than sub-pixel antialiasing at 1280x720 and 720x360.
- Compressed SVG bytes drop by about 18%.

## Decisions

## Outcome

Every Art producer writes its SVG coordinates through one output step at one decimal and minifies
it; the sets that need more decimals are listed with their reasons in `art/README.md`. Over the
7,633 generated SVGs, raw bytes fall 12.4% (21.9 to 19.2 MB) and deflate-9 bytes 13.6% (6.09 to
5.27 MB), short of the expected 18%; the ship set falls 37.4% compressed and the whales 2.9%.
