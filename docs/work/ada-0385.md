## Scope

Redesign the enemy ships from scratch, on the principles of the station redesign: clean lines, simple large shapes, mostly flat colours, functional detail only, and forms that read as connected solids. References: the X-wing and Y-wing of Star Wars, the Cardassian and Klingon ships of Star Trek, and the ships of Battlestar Galactica and The Expanse. Mock-ups in several styles come first; the user directs the changes, and variations follow once a reference is settled.

## Criteria

- Mock-ups cover every enemy class at game scale on the galaxy fields, with Ada's ship for size.
- Production starts only after the user settles a reference.

## Decisions

- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-redesign-hit-area): Accept the redesign hit-area changes with no HP or speed retune; playtest interceptor strafing passes and the Manta gunship.
- D2 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-redesign-retaliator-bodies): Retaliators roll Manta gunship, Cargo Hauler and Mining Freighter with equal weight in every galaxy; the freighters keep their own idle patrols.
- D3 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-redesign-flush-ports): Small craft keep independent aim; flush hull ports are where shots start.
- D4 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-redesign-placement-radius): Spacing and footprint radius is the larger of half the drawn length and half the drawn width.
