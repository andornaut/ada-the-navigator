## Scope

Set the direct ammo-pack odds for ordinary small, medium, large and huge
asteroids to 1%, 3%, 6% and 9%, respectively. Set the rate to 15% for each
eligible destroyed fighter, including escorts, swarm craft, interceptor and
retaliator.

## Criteria

- Preserve owned non-Laser weapon eligibility, crystal suppression and the
  asteroid's exclusive choice between ammo and resources. Keep the scoped
  heavy direct ammo rate at zero.
- Preserve existing ammo pack selection and quantities, asteroid resource
  floors, and small-ship Stardust and upgrade rolls.
- Check the exact odds boundaries and unchanged eligibility. Pair the
  checked source with the current economy rules and normal delivery.
