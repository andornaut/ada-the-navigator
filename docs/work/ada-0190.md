## Scope

Show upgrade levels on Ada's jets, weapon discs, shield bar length and item notices.

## Criteria

- Level 2 is orange and level 3 purple on the jets and weapon disc edges, with one to three pips so level never relies on colour alone.
- Upgrade item notices take the tier's rim and each kind's 20 px shop icon; a paint scheme takes its own top colour.
- `ship-jets.json` keeps only `engineLevels` for Ada's jets.

## Decisions

- Tier colours repeat across jets, discs and notices so one upgrade reads the same everywhere.
