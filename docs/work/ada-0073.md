## Scope

Select galaxies on a route map over the route sky, open the galaxy map in flight from the local
map's Galaxies with Stay here, make Earth a destination on it, and hide locked galaxies' names.

## Criteria

- Back and Escape close the galaxy map and focus Galaxies.
- Earth destination and map close follow distinct story routes.
- A locked action reads Progress the story to unlock, with no future name.

## Decisions

- The selector has one named primary destination action and no outer frame.
- Request `locked-galaxy-action-copy`.
