## Scope

Present the existing update confirmation as soon as a verified update is
ready, including during active flight.

## Criteria

- Freeze flight and make underlying controls inert while the offer is open.
- Show pending verification until the update is staged. Keep explicit Apply
  and let Cancel continue on the active build.
- Qualify a cached offer at startup and a successor staged during flight.
