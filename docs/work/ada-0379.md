## Scope

Place asteroids in one pass, then give a portion of eligible placed rocks
velocity. Support both individual movers and groups of nearby rocks.

## Criteria

- Select movers from rocks whose positions already passed placement checks.
- Check moving paths against existing launch, arrival and protected-area rules.
- Compare moving-asteroid sightings in the game view along matched normal flight
  routes before and after the change.

## Decisions

- Started 2026-11-25 at the user's request.

## Outcome

Audited against the ada-0380 planner switch (f18e18b12).

- Movers come from placed rocks: met. The planner's moving pass
  (`src/model/spawnPlanner.ts`, `moving`) must follow the rock pass, and
  chooses only accepted placements of the `from` kinds (`rock-feature`, `rock`)
  in the same chunk that also clear the zones that apply to moving rocks.
  `spawnPlanner.test.ts` "chooses moving rocks from the placed rocks without
  changing them" and `flightSpawns.test.ts` "loads exactly the planner's
  placements in every chunk along a route" cover it.
- Individual movers and groups of nearby rocks: the planner supports both, but
  the shipped densities never form a group. With two or more movers in a chunk,
  `ceil(count * groupShare)` of them, the rocks nearest a seeded anchor, share
  one velocity and the rest move solo (`spawnPlanner.test.ts` "chooses moving
  rocks ..." and "moves a group of a chunk's rocks nearest one of them"). The
  mover count is drawn per chunk from the table density over nine chunks, at
  most 3.66 / 9 = 0.41, so every chunk draws 0 or 1 mover. In play no group
  forms, against the rule that about 75% of moving rocks travel in groups. Not
  met: it needs a Gameplay rule for the size of a group or the area a group is
  drawn over.
- Paths checked against launch, arrival and protected areas: met after a fix.
  Each mover's straight path over `MOVING_PATH_SECONDS` (30 s, up to 2400 world
  units) is checked against zones that apply to paths: station protected areas
  and the safe radius at the flight start and each Travel arrival
  (`worldSpawnExclusions`), and placed stations and destinations
  (`flightSpawnExclusions`). A blocked path redraws the velocity up to 24 times
  (`spawnPlanner.test.ts` "redraws a moving rock's velocity until its path keeps
  clear of path zones"). Each chunk queried its zones only out to
  `chunkSpawnReach`, 1276 to 1444 world units, shorter than a path, so 17 of
  4322 movers planned near the launch point and stations reached an area the
  planner never saw. `chunkSpawnReach` now also covers half a chunk diagonal,
  the longest checked path and the largest rock (`spawnTargets.test.ts` "moving
  rock paths").

Moving-rock sightings along matched routes, before (main 80fce6a43, the
ballistic spawner) and after (f18e18b12). A sighting is a moving rock whose
footprint enters the 720 x 360 world-unit view centred on Ada, counted once per
rock per route. Ada flies x 4000 to 40000 at 200 world units per second in
0.1 s steps: seeds 101, 202 and 303 at y 1500, and seed 101 at y 12000, in each
galaxy. A rock counts as grouped when another loaded mover shares its velocity.
