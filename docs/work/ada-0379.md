## Scope

Place asteroids in one pass, then give a portion of eligible placed rocks
velocity. Support both individual movers and groups of nearby rocks.

## Criteria

- Select movers from rocks whose positions already passed placement checks.
- Check moving paths against existing launch, arrival and protected-area rules.
- Compare moving-asteroid sightings in the game view along matched normal flight
  routes before and after the change.

## Decisions

- Started 2026-11-25 at the user's request.
- User, 2026-11-25: double the moving-rock targets in every galaxy and region,
  move rocks at 10 to 100 world units per second, and replace per-chunk groups
  with small clusters. Gameplay set the values in gameplay/rules.md.

## Outcome

- Movers come from placed rocks: met. The planner's moving pass
  (`src/model/spawnPlanner.ts`, `moving`) must follow the rock pass and chooses
  only accepted placements of the `from` kinds (`rock-feature`, `rock`) in the
  same chunk that clear the zones that apply to moving rocks.
  `spawnPlanner.test.ts` "chooses moving rocks from the placed rocks without
  changing them" and `flightSpawns.test.ts` "loads exactly the planner's
  placements in every chunk along a route" cover it.
- Individual movers and clusters: met. A seeded half of chosen rocks lead a
  cluster, pulling 1 to 3 (equal weight) of the nearest free slot or feature
  rocks within 300 world units at the leader's velocity; the rest move alone.
  Every rock that moves counts toward the target, so a chunk draws its chosen
  rocks over the mean number one choice moves there, each cluster size capped
  by the rocks near the chosen one (`rocksPerChoice`). A leader pulls only rocks
  of its own chunk, since each chunk plans its rocks alone, so fewer qualify
  than the rule's world-wide nearest rocks would give: 60% of moving rocks
  travel in clusters, against the rule's about 75%. The per-chunk groups at
  f18e18b12 never formed, since every chunk drew at most one mover. Values live
  in `MOVING_ROCKS` (`src/data/spawnTargets.ts`); the ricochet cap is its top
  speed. `spawnPlanner.test.ts` "pulls each cluster from its leader's nearest
  free rocks within the cluster radius" and "leads clusters with a seeded share
  of chosen rocks and counts every mover", and `spawnTargets.test.ts` "moving
  rock values", cover it.
- Paths checked against launch, arrival and protected areas: met. Each mover's
  straight path over 30 s at its speed, up to 3000 world units, is checked
  against station protected areas and the safe radius at the flight start and
  each Travel arrival (`worldSpawnExclusions`), and placed stations and
  destinations (`flightSpawnExclusions`); a blocked path redraws the velocity up
  to 24 times. Each chunk queried its zones only out to 1276 to 1444 world
  units, shorter than a path, so 17 of 4322 movers planned near the launch
  point and stations reached an area the planner never saw. `chunkSpawnReach`
  now covers half a chunk diagonal, the path seconds times the top speed and
  the largest rock (`spawnTargets.test.ts` "moving rock paths").

Spawn acceptance passes in every galaxy and region. Moving rocks deliver in at
least 99.4% of sampled areas, at 0.89 to 1.11 times the table density per area;
the lowest kind is Phantom inner swarms at 94.1%.
