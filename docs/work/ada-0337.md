## Scope

Write varied dialogue for points of interest in all six galaxies. Give Ada, Penny
and Astra distinct approaches, reactions and ambient conversations. Match each
line to the place, the galaxy and what the characters know.

## Criteria

- Approach dialogue plays while the player is heading toward the relevant
  place. Lines about unconfirmed details remain speculative. A line that has
  lost its context must not start or resume.
- Reactions follow the actual scan or confirmed discovery promptly. Keep
  factual receipts, quest updates and warnings in their existing order and
  preserve distinct confirmations that happen in the same update.
- Ambient conversations are spaced out and connect threads within the current
  galaxy. Talk about known places as discoveries and unseen ones as possibilities.
- Named places have multiple variants. Generated locations use galaxy-specific
  variants appropriate to their kind. Variation across runs preserves factual
  hints, quest receipts and prior discovery progress.
- Check coverage and the timing of all three modes in play, including a lost
  approach, a scan, simultaneous confirmations, interrupted dialogue and a
  reference to a previously discovered place. The checked source follows
  normal delivery.
