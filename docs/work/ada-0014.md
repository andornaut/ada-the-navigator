## Scope

Match `art/map.md`, `art/layout.md` and `art/ui.md` to the local map and galaxy selector source.

## Criteria

- The local map has three tools in a row, a footer with Quests, Galaxies and the primary Close ("Back" from Paused), and no close X.
- The map header starts `SPACING.gap` right of the pause press area, fits within `mapHeaderCap`, and centres its row with equal gaps. The survey note is `TYPE.body` supporting `#b8b3c9`.
- An empty danger panel shows the note then the danger line, both centred, 80 high.
- The galaxy selector has no close X, an Earth marker, and one primary galaxy-jump action (192x48). Tab order runs markers, Earth, the action, then wraps. Back and Escape go one step back.

## Decisions

- Source is authoritative for the tool order and the footer Close; the docs follow it.
- The header clears the pause disc and the note fits the cap, so nothing clips at 844x390.

## Outcome

Specified on `art-map-tools-source` (450d8622). Art reviews the Earth marker and label contrast on the route sky from Tech's galaxy map captures.
