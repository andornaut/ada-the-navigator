## Scope

The runtime reads the ada-0290 debris rules: the speck `cell` and `alpha`, and the `rocks` and
`farRocks` depths (`parallax`, `salt`, `cell`, `chance`, `scale`, `value`). It draws both rock
depths, far rocks between the streaks and the rocks, each muted toward the galaxy's field
`background` to its `value` while staying opaque. Planets and far items whose drawn extent misses
the view are dropped before baking.

## Criteria

- `backdropRock` takes a depth rule; with salt `backdrop` the near rocks keep their places.
- Each rock draws as a background-filled silhouette under the `backdropLit` rock at alpha
  `value`, the pair at one depth in its band, rocks of a depth back to front by projected y.
- A frame within the camera's cells creates no images and seeds no cells.
- At most four planets and four far items meet the view and its margin.
- The browser smoke captures every galaxy at 1280x720 and 720x360 with muted near rocks drawn.

## Decisions

- Two draws per rock over a baked muted texture: tint mode is a per-vertex attribute, so the
  silhouette shares the rock's texture and batch, adding no baked textures, no memory and no
  rebake on galaxy entry or raster rescale. The cost is one extra quad and its fill per rock.
- Non-ringed planets are culled by their disc, ringed ones by their ring extent. Up to four
  items meet the view in a 4000-camera sweep, so the bound is four, not three.

## Outcome

Both depths draw in every galaxy with no baked rock textures. A flight frame with no play objects
takes 11 to 13 WebGL draw calls in every galaxy, the same at 1280x720 and 720x360.
