## Scope

Keep the discovery notice visible for a few seconds, then fade it in place.

## Criteria

- A point-of-interest discovery follows a readable dwell and short fade.
- The notice keeps its existing placement and input behavior while visible.
  Warnings, scan receipts, overlays and other conversations retain their
  priority and timing.
- Verify the discovered state, dwell, fade and removal in the rendered game.
  The checked source follows normal delivery.

## Decisions

## Outcome

Discovery lines stay visible for three seconds. The final line fades in place
over 300 ms, with a shorter opacity ramp in reduced motion. The notice uses the
existing placement and controls. Warnings and scan receipts keep their existing
order and timing.

In Chromium, approaching a previously unknown friendly station showed the first
line for 3.024 seconds. The final line was fully opaque at 2.900 seconds, faded
to 60% opacity at 3.124 seconds without moving, and was hidden at 3.316 seconds.
The checked revision reached production in `8195ff03f` on 2026-11-22.
