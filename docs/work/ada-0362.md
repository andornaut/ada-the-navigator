## Scope

Spend one Multi-Laser ammo per emitted Ada bolt. If there is too little ammo
for a full fan, favor the existing angles closest to the aim direction.

## Criteria

- A full volley spends three, four or five ammo by tier. A short volley
  spends exactly the bolts emitted with a stable tie break.
- The last volley fires once and falls back to Laser with the existing
  out-of-ammo notice.
- Preserve full-fan angles, damage, cadence, ammo offers, other weapons
  and enemy volleys.
