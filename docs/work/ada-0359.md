## Scope

Arrange the in-flight Pause card in two top-aligned columns. Use the shared
Achievements screen from Pause and retain its own parent return.

## Criteria

- The left column sorts its available actions by label. In English these are
  Cheats, Map when available, Quests and Ship.
- The right column contains Achievements, Settings, Back to title and Resume
  in that order. Resume keeps the primary face.
- Every button uses its column's full width and the standard button height.
  The columns keep their positions when Map is absent.
- Back, Close and Escape from Achievements restore Pause and its Achievements
  focus target. The title-screen Achievements flow stays independent.
