## Scope

Tapping the in-flight conversation box dismisses the whole conversation. Every shield hit flashes a 3 px red perimeter once for about 200 ms and shakes the view about 2 px for about 100 ms. Both sticks sit 50 from the game area's outer side and 50 above its bottom, ignoring safe insets, moving inward only to stay 8 inside. The local map has Zoom out, Zoom in and Center on Ada and no close X, with a footer Back when opened from Paused and Close otherwise. The galaxy map has no close X or Back button: Ada leaves it through the destination action, or back and Escape go one step back.

## Criteria

- `gameplay/rules.md` states each rule on `main`.
- The rules match the integrated source for the conversation box, hit feedback, stick placement and both maps.

## Decisions

- Reduced motion disables both the flash and the shake.
- The Use disc keeps its offset from the aim stick, and the shield pill keeps its own bottom line.
- The map rules follow the source behaviour found in Tech's smoke tests.
