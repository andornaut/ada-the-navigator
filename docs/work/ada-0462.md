## Criteria

- One Ore funds 2% of maximum shield capacity at all Shield levels.
- Keep the fractional credit in existing saves at its original value. New
  saves can store up to 2% of unused capacity credit.
- Preserve the recharge timer, rate, free floor, emergency jump and cumulative
  spending notices.
