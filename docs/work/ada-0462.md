## Criteria

- One Ore funds 2% of maximum shield capacity at all Shield levels.
- Keep the fractional credit in existing saves at its original value. New
  saves can store up to 2% of unused capacity credit.
- Preserve the recharge timer, rate, free floor, emergency jump and cumulative
  spending notices.

## Outcome

One Ore funds 2% of maximum shield capacity at all three levels, so 50
Ore funds a full bar. Save round trips restored existing and new paid
fractions at their original values and consumed them without billing
again. The checked source shipped in build `7d0bf2d8`.
