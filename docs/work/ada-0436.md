## Scope

Show the tracked quest's flight marker without a label or extra badge. Indicate
which quest is tracked in the quest log. Let the player open details from every
visible flight marker.

## Criteria

- A quest marker opens the associated quest details. Other markers open details
  appropriate to their current identity and discovery state.
- Tap, hold and keyboard activation use the same action. Dismissing details
  returns to the previous flight state.
- Preserve the single tracked quest, marker placement, reduced motion, and
  existing discovery and interaction restrictions.
