## Scope

Show loading on a bar along the top edge that fills when progress is known, travels when it is not
and is absent when idle, only for loads the player waits for.

## Criteria

- The bar draws 3 high above every layer and fades after its hold.
- Offline installs and verified worker downloads report on the same bar.
- Its scale is consistent from first paint.

## Decisions

- game-kit's progress bar draws the alphas and fade.
