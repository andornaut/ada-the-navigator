## Scope

Restyle the landing actions and modals with Art's controls, fit every card to its content with
Chibi's shared card layout, move navigation and Settings into Chibi's pause button and Paused card,
and guard browser back as Chibi does.

## Criteria

- Cards fit their content within Chibi's 16 px padding.
- Back closes the innermost open card; on the start screen it leaves to the browser.
- The refusal cue is shared.
