## Scope

Use the travel animation to prepare the destination's visible area. Release
flight display objects when Ada leaves a flight.

## Criteria

- Start destination art requests after an accepted Jump or Travel action.
  Keep the destination covered during its initial draw and bound waits on
  optional art.
- Preserve travel admission, costs, save progress and the source presentation
  during the emergency return warning.
- Verify repeated galaxy transitions release old display instances. Compare
  the first rendered frames in Phantom separately from sustained flight.

## Outcome

Confirmed Jump and Travel prepare destination art under the transition cover.
The checked build keeps waits for optional art bounded, retains admission and
cost guards, and preserves the flight state and emergency return warning.

Across five successive galaxy jumps in one Chromium session, the previous
flight's tracked display instances were destroyed or inactive and absent from
the current maps. The first Phantom arrival measured 150.1 ms p95 over its
first 20 visible frames and 33.4 ms over the last 40. The return measured
33.4 ms in both windows. The visits started in different galaxies.

The preparation and cleanup shipped. The observed cold Phantom hitch and
phone performance remain tracked under [flight performance](ada-0402.md)
and [resume and current hitches](ada-0426.md).
