## Scope

Shield recharge keeps its ten-ore cost ticks under the status pill and, when the recharge ends,
shows the remaining paid cost of 1 to 10 once.

## Criteria

- Cost ticks stay batched in tens.
- When the recharge ends, its remaining paid cost shows exactly once.
- Charged ore totals do not change and no tick repeats.

## Decisions

- The tick reports ore already paid; it never changes what recharge charges.
