## Scope

Use the reviewed role silhouettes and palettes for the small combat craft,
frigates, capitals, cargo hauler, mining freighter, and passive rescue craft.

## Criteria

- Keep existing craft and mount identities, role and size ratios, and ship
  behaviors. Use the paired hull, weapon, outline, and tow assets.
- Verify the selected palette and shape at gameplay size. Check damage,
  weapon contact, and towing against the matching asset data.
- Keep concurrent station manifest entries when composing the art. Run the
  combined checks and follow normal delivery.
