## Scope

Apply the approved enemy speed and pursuit changes within the existing behavior
system and pair them with the Gameplay rules.

## Criteria

- Top speeds in world units per second are 175 for swarms, 160 for fighters and
  escorts, 240 for interceptors, 120 for retaliators, 100 for frigates, and 50 for
  capitals and the finale flagship. Stations and platforms stay stationary.
- Free-roaming swarms and fighters chase within 2,500 world units of home,
  interceptors within 3,000, frigates within 1,500, and retaliators, capitals,
  and the finale flagship within 1,000. Preserve guard, escort, and authored
  encounter limits except for the approved finale flagship change.
- Use the last visible position for the eight-second sight-loss window. Preserve
  the 30-second out-of-range budget, two-second rearm, and existing siege speed
  fractions and peaceful industrial route extents.
- A visible, engaged interceptor gives direct chase when Ada is moving outward
  and opening the gap beyond firing range. Preserve the normal attack pass in
  range, and respect toroidal distance and existing target eligibility.
- Verify approach, sight loss and return, role exceptions, wrapped pursuit,
  authored limits, and Continue with the matching Gameplay rules.

## Decisions

## Outcome

Enemy top speeds and home limits now match the Gameplay rules above. The
tests cover wrapped interceptor pursuit, the last visible target and eight-second
cutoff, authored role exceptions, and saved combat state through Continue.
The exact model and Gameplay pair passed the full gate, and `f0b33178`
reached production on 2026-11-23 UTC.
