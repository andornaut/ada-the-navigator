## Scope

Encode the quantum jump field and tunnel sheets as lossy WebP at quality 90 to cut the download size. The glow and star sheets stay PNG.

## Criteria

- `public/art/quantum-jump-field.webp` and `public/art/quantum-jump-tunnel.webp` are lossy WebP at quality 90, produced by `art/tools/quantum-jump.ts`.
- `art/quantum-jump.md` records the encoding of each sheet.
- The rendered jump shows no visible banding or edge artefacts against the lossless sheets.

## Decisions

- Quality 90 over lossless: the field sheet drops from 669,066 to 64,060 bytes and the tunnel sheet from 845,166 to 109,126, with no visible loss in motion.

## Outcome

Produced on `art-quantum-q90` (61fc957c).
