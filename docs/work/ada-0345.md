## Scope

Ship the reviewed station silhouettes and point of interest art. Give the
smaller stations more presence, make berths read as part of the hull,
retain the Eddy spiral, and give the Workshop and distress beacon their
revised silhouettes.

## Criteria

- Use the selected Art source and its generated manifests for the six
  station families, affected outposts, beacon and discovery cache.
  Preserve asset identities, animation order and reduced motion.
- Pair the funded Cartwheel berth overlay with the matching visitor
  position. Preserve the unfunded buoy wait, both side berths and
  project progression. Check rendered hull clearance during approach
  and after the visitor docks.
- Verify the enlarged stations at their existing world anchors, including
  Use, callouts and clearance. Check the revised cache silhouette against
  its collision sidecar and keep the station safety rules.
- Review the rendered assets and run the combined checks before normal
  delivery.
