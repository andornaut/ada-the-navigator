## Scope

Show a near-black background beyond the full playable galaxy's projected
boundary in the Local Map. The centre survey oval remains its own marker.
Review the visual mock-up before implementation.

## Criteria

- The background follows the full wrapped world's projected edge when panning
  or zooming. Its boundary matches the playable field.
- Playable map content and controls retain their existing visuals and actions.
