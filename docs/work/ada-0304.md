## Scope

From Ada's room, the player can replay an Earth chapter already finished. A choice made in a
replay replaces the saved choice and its outcome.

## Criteria

- A replay choice replaces the saved choice and outcome.
- Rewards follow the new choice and are never paid twice.
- Skip returns to the room.
- A replay never consumes pending mandatory story.

## Decisions

- Replays may change choices (user, 2026-11-18).
- Feasibility and implementation are not yet assessed.
