## Scope

After the existing Ore reduction and both resource scales, require combined
Ore and Stardust minima of 0, 1, 2 and 3 for small, medium, large and huge
asteroids. Count crystal Ore toward the minimum. Fill only an unmet minimum
with seeded resource units using the current 75% Ore and 25% Stardust split.

## Criteria

- Keep combined maxima of 4, 6, 8 and 10 and preserve the crystal Ore guarantee.
- Preserve ordinary payouts already inside their bounds, exclusive ammo
  replacement, ammo odds and eligibility, and five-unit Ore bundles.
- Moving and stationary forms of the same rock keep the same drop identity
  after saving and continuing.
- Check final payouts and pair the measured expected yields with the economy
  rules before normal delivery.
