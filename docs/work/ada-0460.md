## Criteria

- Use authored choices and stable branch and return edges. Ask Wick what is
  happening to the ships, preserve the explanation about the beacons and
  remove the duplicate question. Preserve Previous, read topics and
  one-time outcomes.
- Keep passive flight dialogue in its overlay. Open a fresh point-of-interest
  conversation through contextual Use. Preserve explicit Continue and
  required story effects.
- In range of a genuine point of interest, an explicit Use resolves the
  available action, applicable details or refusal, then a relevant hint.
  Hints never appear from approach alone, reveal undiscovered information or
  change quest progress.
- Put Talk first in the existing shell for stations with services. Preserve
  the dialogue page, history and read state across service tabs. Reset that
  visit state on exit. Keep standalone conversation for a place without
  services.
- Check the real Use button, early and late quest visits, available actions,
  and switching services and returning to Talk without duplicate outcomes.

## Outcome

Explicit Use opens the available place action, gives an applicable refusal
or quest detail, or shows an exploration hint. Approaching alone keeps the
existing flight dialogue overlay. Chromium exercised the available scan,
choice navigation, the early Andromeda hint, and a refused Needle holodisk
press with nine of ten Eye gates lit. The refusal left quest state unchanged.

Station Talk retains its page and read topics while Ada visits other service
tabs. Leaving resets the visit, and quest outcomes commit once. Places
without services keep standalone dialogue. Model and native checks covered
Wick, Cartwheel, and the Whirlpool whale exchange.

Elder Loom keeps the authored disk action. Previous follows the visited
history and leaves committed outcomes intact. Earth keeps its optional
questions and Take the seat. The dialogue and its dismissal control stay
inside the checked landscape safe area. Touch scrolling exposes Earth’s
remaining question while the footer stays in place.
