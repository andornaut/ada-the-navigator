## Scope

Every launch boards and selects a galaxy. Six galaxies lie on one arc over a night sky; placeholder
galaxies open on completing a galaxy, one unlock flies to the farthest, and the map is left only by
Earth, Back or Escape. The local map's Galaxies opens it, with Stay here for the current galaxy.
