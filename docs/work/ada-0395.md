## Scope

Enemy ships come to rest in several states, so two ships can sit still side by
side. Every ship that can move keeps moving at least at a low speed: patrolling,
or for miners, tracing the edges of nearby asteroids.

## Criteria

- Every state in which a movable enemy stops is identified with its cause.
- In each such state the ship keeps at least a minimum speed, on a patrol or,
  for mining craft, along nearby asteroid edges.
- Ships that wait for line of sight or hold a station keep moving while they
  wait.
- Values are authored data.
- Tests cover each former resting state.

## Decisions

- All ships move at least at a low speed at all times, on a patrol or, for
  miners, along asteroid edges (user, 2026-11-25).
- D1 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0395-min-speed): Every movable enemy keeps at least 0.25 of its current top speed (after engine loss and whale-song slowdown).
- D2 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0395-packs): Idle packs fly one shared seeded route per pack, members in order and spaced apart.
- D3 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0395-scope): Always-moving covers enemy ships only; story ships, allies and whales keep their waiting behaviour.
- D4 (user, 2026-11-25; answers ada-0395-standdown): After the flagship is salvaged, Phantom drones patrol home without firing.

## Outcome

Implemented in Tech on `ada-0395-keep-moving`, rebased on main, with Gameplay's
rules patches (`gameplay/ships.md` "Keep moving" and "Miners", and the
follow-up answering Tech's questions) applied first.

- Resting causes found in seeded flights (six galaxies, four seeds, 90 s each,
  about 10,000 ship-seconds; a rest is under 5% of top speed for over 2 s):
  obstacle steering with no clear course returned a zero request, and a
  position-only patrol, search or return course never changed while the ship
  stood still (75 rests, including engaged ships searching a last-seen point
  beside a rock); peer contact zeroed velocity on the blocked axis and patrols
  had no separation, so crossing routes locked ships side by side (37); an
  escort arriving on its station (1); a platform (1, expected). 52 of the 115
  rests had another ship within 3 world units. The Phantom stand-down froze
  every drone, and a blockade's 600 reach pinned a ship pressing outward.
  After the change the same flights show one rest, the platform.
- Authored data: `ENEMY_KEEP_MOVING` and `MINING_HULLS` in
  `src/data/enemyNavigation.ts` hold the 0.25 floor, the 2r turret hold, the
  20 pack and edge gaps, the 6 to 12 s dwell and the 3 recent picks; the
  galaxy's member spacing comes from `SPAWNER_POLICIES`.
- Model (`src/model/enemyAi.ts`, `src/model/ai/packRoute.ts`,
  `src/model/ai/edgeTrace.ts`): the request floor; noncombat courses use peer
  separation; packs fly one seeded route through their homes' mean or a
  last-seen point, spaced in id order by the larger of each ship's spacing and
  collision radius; escorts circle the leader's station
  ring, turning with the leader's age, and keep their nose on Ada while they
  see her; siege heavies circle Ada at 2r once close; a cancelled band hold
  strafes; obstacle steering with no clear course reverses and flips a solo
  patrol; the Mining Freighter picks the nearest qualifying rock outside its
  last 3 picks, travels to it at its patrol pace, traces its drawn outline at
  the minimum speed and dwells from its arrival. Escort rings turn at the
  minimum speed plus the leader's current top pace. Settled on a large test
  rock, its centre held 50 to 73 world units from the outline, 60 on average,
  against the 50 (r + 20) target.
- Runtime (`src/model/flight.ts`): a powered ship that a peer or protected
  area blocks slides along the contact at least at the minimum speed, moving
  on both axes at once; head-on peers turn the same way and pass, a ship
  wedged between contacts leaves along their mean direction, overlapping ships
  may separate, a blockade ship turns along its reach, Phantom's drones stand down to a home patrol without fire,
  and the miner's qualifying rocks and traced edge are supplied, its traced
  rock checked at half the edge gap instead of the berths. Enemy saves keep
  `miner` (rock, arrival time, pick count and last 3 picks) and `patrolFlip`.
- The Mining Freighter's old asteroid-anchored ellipse is removed.
- The deployed `1a9d0d2a` artifact preserved these movement rules. The checked
  flight cases confirm that a combined two-axis contact step can separate the
  ships when the X step alone closes the gap, and that a wedged ship follows
  the mean of its contact normals. Separate mutations failed the corresponding
  assertions. Native Phantom Salvage also showed powered escorts continuing
  to patrol without firing across reload and Continue.
