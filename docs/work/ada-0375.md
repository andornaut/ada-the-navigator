## Scope

Use the same inclusive Stardust ranges for each enemy archetype in every galaxy.

## Criteria

- Roll whole amounts uniformly. Swarms give 1 to 3, fighters, escorts,
  interceptors and retaliators give 2 to 5, frigates and turret platforms give
  4 to 10, and ordinary capitals give 10 to 20.
- Salvaging a frigate, turret platform or ordinary capital pays half of its
  rolled destruction amount, rounded down.
- Pay for an encounter once. Keep the rolled amount stable through save and
  Continue and preserve existing upgrade and ammunition odds and rolls.
- Keep hostile-station awards, the finale flagship's 44 Stardust, and loot
  from sources other than enemies.

## Decisions

The final fighter and escort range is 2 to 5. The final capital range is 10 to 20. Both use the same rules across galaxies.

- D1 (user, 2026-11-24; answers ada-enemy-stardust-salvage): Half the rolled payout, rounded down (Recommended)

## Outcome

The fixed ranges and shared destruction and salvage roll shipped in
`d11fac53` and remain in `1a9d0d2a`. The model checks cover the inclusive
integer endpoints, original encounter identity, floor rounding, and no
repeat payout after Continue. The existing upgrade and ammunition rolls,
hostile-station awards and Phantom flagship payout remain in place.
