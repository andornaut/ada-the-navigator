## Scope

Place POI scan and weapon change notices below Ada's ship and above her shield
meter. Center them between the flight controls. If both are visible, stack the
scan receipt above the weapon notice.

## Criteria

- Check short and long receipts and weapon names against the lower controls
  and shield at compact and wide play sizes, with all weapons equipped.
- Show the selected weapon's existing icon to the left of its name.
- Preserve boss and dialogue priority, safe insets, and the existing hold and
  fade timing.
