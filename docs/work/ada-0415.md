## Scope

Unlock missing weapons and refill every finite weapon ammo supply to capacity
each time Ada uses the cheat.

## Criteria

- Enable the action when any weapon is missing or any finite ammo supply is below
  capacity. Disable it once Ada owns every weapon and all supplies are full.
- Preserve weapon levels, current selection and Stardust. The refill persists
  through Continue.
