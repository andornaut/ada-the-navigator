## Scope

Apply the ada-0279 denser muted backdrop parameters in the backdrop producer for all six galaxies. Tech adds runtime support for the far rock depth and opaque rock muting.

## Criteria

- The producer emits the ada-0279 parameters; art/backdrop.md states them.
- Worst-case keyline and HUD ink contrast is at least the current value in every galaxy.
- Rendered proofs per galaxy at 1280x720 and 720x360 match the mock-up direction.

## Decisions

## Outcome

The denser backdrop producer and specification landed in `2b7acbcaa`. The
two rock depths and opaque muting landed in `0f4f0e2cf` and shipped with the
producer in `03f32e28`. Art reviewed all 12 Chromium captures from
`e03089df9` and confirmed the dense muted direction in all six galaxies at
1280x720 and 720x360.

The retained Art compose comparisons supply the contrast ratios. Each
keyline floor is `min(viewKeyline, spriteRingKeyline)` and each HUD ink floor
is `min(hudInkView, hudPanelEdge)`. Both floors improve in every galaxy.
