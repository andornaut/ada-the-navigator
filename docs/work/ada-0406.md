## Scope

Keep part of the centre charting oval on screen when the Local Map is fully
zoomed out.

## Criteria

- Extreme horizontal, vertical and diagonal pans at maximum zoom-out leave
  a visible segment of the projected charting oval.
- Entering maximum zoom-out near a seam moves the view into the allowed
  region through the shared pan and zoom limit.
- Keep the existing world size, zoom range and closer-scale pan limits.
- Verify the actual Map controls through the normal gate.

## Outcome

Deployed in `7b8c1f1a`. At maximum zoom-out, the shared pan and zoom
limit leaves part of the charting oval in the map field. The world size, zoom
range and closer pan limits stay the same. All six galaxy bounds passed the
normal gate.
