# Cheats

The [shared saves and cheats policy](https://github.com/andornaut/game-coordinator/blob/main/docs/game-policies.md#saves-and-cheats) applies.
The user approved this list on 2026-11-18.

Choose **Cheats** directly from Menu or Pause in a browser tab or installed
app. The modal has two unlabeled columns with three aligned rows each. The
left column has the three unlock controls. The right column has Stardust and Ore
side by side, Maximize weapon levels, then Reset jobs, projects & quests.
The centered primary **Back** button and top-right X return to the invoking
Menu or Pause and restore focus to **Cheats**.

The **FPS counter** switch is at the top of the right column in Settings. The
choice persists on this device with the other Settings preferences. Its readout
stays at the bottom right of the device viewport, inside the safe area and
above the game and letterbox bands.

## Save

Cheats act on the real save like normal play. Cheats, and play after them,
save, earn and bank as usual: story progress, unlocks, ownership, levels,
Stardust, ore, jobs, achievements and statistics. Returning to the start screen
or reopening the game keeps cheated progress, and Continue loads the real save.
An unlock marks nothing cleared: no chapter, holodisk, quest, landmark or
project counts as done.

Cheats also work on the start screen and apply to the next Start or Continue.
**Restart** on the start screen returns to a new save;
[What each event keeps](../../gameplay/rules.md#what-each-event-keeps) lists
what it clears and keeps.

## Cheats

- **Unlock all weapons** makes Ada own Multi-Laser, Rapid Missile, Heavy
  Missile, Scattershot and Phaser and refills each weapon with a finite ammo
  supply to capacity. Levels, selection and Stardust are unchanged. Disabled
  when Ada owns every weapon and every ammo supply is full.
- **Unlock all equipment** sets Shield, Engine tuning and Tractor beam to level
  3 and makes Ada own every ship paint; the selected paint stays. Disabled once
  all are at level 3 and every paint is owned.
- **Unlock all galaxies** unlocks all six route positions, so the galaxy map can
  jump to any of them. Story progress, holodisks, chapters and quests are
  unchanged, and the next boarding plays the route flight to the farthest newly
  unlocked galaxy. Disabled once every galaxy is unlocked.
- **Maximize weapon levels** sets every owned weapon, Laser included, to level 3.
  Disabled once every owned weapon is at level 3.
- **Add 1,000 ore** adds 1,000 to Ada's ore balance on every press.
- **Add 1,000 Stardust** adds 1,000 Stardust on every press.

## Other actions

- **Reset jobs, projects & quests** asks for confirmation before rewinding the
  stored campaign to the Earth opening of Sunflower and relocking later galaxies.
  It clears jobs, project funding and reveals, story progress, and holodisks.
  Ada keeps her ship, equipment, resources, map discoveries, and achievements.
  Past contributions stay spent. Already-earned one-time rewards stay recorded,
  and new repeatable jobs and activities still pay their usual rewards.
  Cancel or X returns to Cheats. A failed write leaves the save and current view
  in place.
