# Prototype dialogue and objectives

Working player text for the three designed chapters. Speaker labels identify
roles rather than final names. Choices converge; optional questions never gate
completion. The text can be split across short dialogue panels.

## Prologue

Plays once, after chapter one's home opening.

Narration: "On a walk with Archie, Ada digs up an unusual rock. At home, she
polishes it until a faint pattern begins to glow."

Choice: "Touch the glow."

Narration: "Light swirls around Ada and Archie. They are standing in a tiny
cockpit. Outside, colours shimmer like nowhere at all."

Astra: "Navigator detected. Hello. I am Astra, this ship's mind."
Ada: "Where are we?"
Astra: "Between dimensions. While you are aboard, Earth's time waits for you."
Astra: "My memory is damaged. I know only one galaxy. A holodisk holding part of
my memory is somewhere there."
Astra: "I was made to explore and map, but I cannot fly without a navigator. Will
you be mine?"

Optional questions:

- "How did you get to Earth?" Astra: "I don't remember. The holodisk may help."
- "Can Archie come?" Astra: "There are two seats. Please strap him in."
- "How do I get home?" Astra: "Ask me any time you're safe. I'll jump you back to
  the moment you left."

Narration: "Ada sits in the pilot's chair and buckles Archie in beside her."
Astra: "Quantum jump ready."
Choice: "Let's find your holodisk."

Boarding, for later chapters: Astra: "Welcome back, Navigator."

Holodisk inserted: Narration: "Ada slides the holodisk into Astra's drive."
Astra: "Memory restored. I've found new coordinates. A new galaxy is ready."

## Chapter one: the garden

Grandmother: "You've planted those carefully. We may need more room next year."
Ada: "Could we grow things somewhere else too?"
Grandmother: "Someone nearby tried starting a community garden. It hasn't got
going. They needed more help. I can introduce you."

Choices:

- "I'd like to meet them."
- "What kind of help do they need?"

Organizer: "A few people offered, but nobody can look after everything."
Ada: "Maybe there's something I can do."

Space introduction:

Grower: "A visitor! We grow food here, but our equipment needs repairs."
Ada: "Is there anyone who can help?"
Grower: "There's a workshop out here somewhere. We haven't spoken much. Would
you take them some food and ask?"

Workshop: "We can repair that equipment. We're short of parts, though."
Workshop: "The salvagers might have what we need. Take them this tool kit."

Salvager: "Useful tools! We have parts to spare. Our food supplies are running low."
Ada: "I know some growers. You could help each other."
Salvager: "Take these parts to the workshop. We'll contact the growers."

Workshop, delivery complete: "Now we can make those repairs. We'll arrange the
next exchange ourselves. Thank you for introducing us."
Ada: "You each had something the others needed."

Optional questions:

- "What do you grow?" Grower: "Food for our outpost. We could grow more with help."
- "What do you repair?" Workshop: "Tools, ships, growing equipment. Parts help!"
- "What do you find?" Salvager: "Things people have lost or left behind. Plenty
  can be used again."

Home resolution:

Ada: "What jobs would help the garden get started?"
Organizer: "Planting, finding tools, watering... and showing new gardeners what to do."
Grandmother: "I could help people learn."

Choices:

- "Let's ask what people can offer."
- "Could we divide the work into smaller jobs?"

Organizer: "Yes. Someone who can't come every week might still help us start."
Ada: "We don't all have to do the same thing."
Closing: "On planting day, neighbors bring tools, plants and time. The garden
has begun."

Objectives, in order:

1. Explore the galaxy.
2. Take food to the workshop.
3. Take the tool kit to the salvagers.
4. Bring spare parts to the workshop.
5. Collect the holodisk from the outposts.
6. Return home when ready.

## Chapter two: the mining hub

Opening: "At school, Ada notices a child being pushed out of a playground game.
Other children look away."
Ada: "That wasn't right. I wonder who else noticed."

Hub introduction:

Station: "Welcome. Break resource rocks in the field and collect what they release.
Bring your cargo here to deposit it. You can return with a partial load."

First deposit milestone:

Miner: "I was approaching that dock."
Blocking pilot: "Find somewhere else."
Ada: "There are other docks free. Why won't they let that miner through?"

Second deposit milestone:

Miner: "It keeps happening. I've started waiting until they're gone."
Ada: "Would you like some company next time you come in?"
Miner: "Yes. That would help."

Choices when speaking with regular visitors:

- "Have you seen what's happening at the docks?"
- "Would you come in with us next time?"

Visitor: "I've seen it. I didn't know what to do. I'll come with you."

Final deposit milestone:

Ada: "Ready to approach together?"
Miner: "Ready."
Visitor: "There's room for everyone here. Let them dock."
Closing: "The other ships stay nearby. The miner docks and unloads."
Miner: "Thank you for staying. I'll see you here next trip."

Home resolution:

Ada, privately: "I saw what happened. Would you like to join us?"
Child: "I'd like that."

Choices:

- "Ask friends to make room in the game."
- "Sit together and invite friends over."

Friend: "We can help make sure nobody gets pushed out."
Closing: "Ada and her friends make room. When the behavior happens again, they
don't laugh along or leave the child alone."

Optional safety exchange:

Ada: "If anyone threatens or hurts you, we can find an adult you trust together."

Objectives:

- Explore the galaxy.
- Mine resources and deposit cargo at the station.
- Keep mining; get to know the miners when you return.
- Speak with the miner and regular visitors.
- Use the station interaction to join the approach when everyone is ready.
- Buy the holodisk at the hub.
- Return home when ready.

## Chapter three: the whale

Parent: "It's time for the dog's walk."
Ada: "But we were just playing! Nobody has to take me for a walk."
Opening: "The dog waits by the door."

Whale introduction:

Whale: "Can you hear me? I can't get through these rocks."
Ada: "You're... a whale? In space? And you can talk?"
Whale: "Yes. And I could use some help."
Ada: "I'll clear a way."

After debris is cleared:

Whale: "That's better. Thank you. But I don't know where my family went."
Ada: "I'll look for them."

Optional questions:

- "Do you travel together?" Whale: "Yes. We have places we visit together. The
  young ones learn the routes from us."
- "What do you like about traveling?" Whale: "The currents. Familiar voices.
  Finding something I haven't seen before."
- "How will I recognize your family?" Whale: "They'll be looking for me too. Ask
  when you find them."

Family encounter:

Whale family: "Have you seen someone traveling alone? We've been searching."
Ada: "Yes! They're safe. I'll tell them where you are."

Return:

Ada: "I found your family. Here are their coordinates."
Whale: "Now I know where to go. Thank you for listening."
Closing: "The whale departs. Soon, the family is traveling together again."

Home resolution:

Closing: "Ada returns. The dog is still by the door."

Choices:

- "Get the lead."
- "Tell the dog: Let's go exploring."

Ada: "You need to get out and see things too. Come on."
Closing: "Ada gives the dog time to stop, sniff and explore."

Objectives, in order:

1. Explore the galaxy.
2. Shoot the debris blocking the whale.
3. Talk with the whale.
4. Explore to find its family.
5. Return to the whale and share the coordinates.
6. Fight your way to the holodisk.
7. Return home when ready.

## Shared interface text

- Use actions: Talk, Deliver, Deposit, Continue.
- Pause: Pause (button label), Paused, Resume, Travel, Settings, Return home.
- Travel card: Discovered destinations. With none yet: No destinations mapped yet.
- Unavailable in danger: Unavailable while you are in danger.
- Cargo: Cargo full. Deposit at the station when ready.
- Shield depletion: Astra jumped you home to recharge. Your story progress is saved.
- Galaxy four: Story in development.
- Completed story: Chapter complete. You can keep exploring.

Galaxy four has no playable story or invented finale in this prototype. Keep its
unfinished status explicit in development builds.

## Landing page

- Pitch: Explore space. Bring new ideas home.
- Subline and meta description: Join Ada on space adventures and help her community
  back home with what she learns.
- Mobile: Move with the left stick. Aim and fire with the right stick. Tap Use to
  interact.
- Desktop: Move with WASD. Aim and fire with the arrow keys. Press Space to
  interact and Escape to pause.
