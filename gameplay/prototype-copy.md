# Prototype dialogue and objectives

Working player text for the three designed chapters. Speaker labels identify
roles rather than final names. Each space dialogue panel shows one speaker label
and its text; opening, resolution and closing panels show the chapter's panel
title. Choices converge. Ask more shows a speaker's optional line and never gates
completion.

## Prologue

Plays once, after chapter one's home opening. Panel title: The Glowing Rock.
In the cockpit, Ada and Penny stand (the empty cockpit) from their arrival through
the optional questions; from "Ada sits in the pilot's chair and buckles Penny in
beside her." onward they are seated. Boarding in later chapters, Continue and
galaxy selection always show them seated.
A replayed chapter one opening uses the boarding panels instead.

Park page: "On a walk with Penny, Ada digs up an unusual rock."

Ada's room, rock unpolished: "At home, she polishes it until a faint pattern begins
to glow."

Ada's room, rock glowing: "The pattern pulses softly, as if it is waiting for
her." Choice: "Touch the glow."

Narration: "Light swirls around Ada and Penny. They are standing in a tiny
cockpit. Outside, colors shimmer like nowhere at all."

Astra: "Navigator detected. Hello. I am Astra, this ship's mind."
Ada: "Where are we?"
Astra: "Between dimensions. While you are aboard, Earth's time waits for you."
Astra: "My memory is damaged. I know only one galaxy. A holodisk holding part of
my memory is somewhere there."
Astra: "I was made to explore and map, but I can no longer fly without a navigator. Will
you be mine?"

Optional questions:

- "How did you get to Earth?" Astra: "I don't remember. The holodisk may help."
- "Can Penny come?" Astra: "There are two seats. Please strap Penny in."
- "How do I get home?" Astra: "Ask me any time you're safe. I'll jump you back to
  the moment you left."

Narration: "Ada sits in the pilot's chair and buckles Penny in beside her."
Astra: "Quantum jump ready."
Choice: "Let's find your holodisk."

Boarding, for later chapters: Narration: "Ada holds Penny close and touches the
rock's glow." Astra: "Welcome back, Navigator."

Holodisk inserted: Narration: "Ada slides the holodisk into Astra's drive."
Astra: "Memory restored. I've found new coordinates."

Then Astra's memory line for that galaxy:

- Sunflower: "I remember a fleet... and someone giving orders."
- Cartwheel: "I carried that fleet from galaxy to galaxy. But whose fleet?"
- Whirlpool: "General Gadget. I was his navigator. I scattered my holodisks to
  keep them from him."
- Black Eye: written with the finale.

General's broadcasts, in the conversation box when his drones first engage Ada in
each galaxy from Cartwheel on. His name tab reads "Unknown voice" until the
Whirlpool holodisk is inserted, then "General Gadget":

- Cartwheel: Unknown voice: "All drones: find the holodisks. Bring them to me."
  Astra: "That voice... I don't know why it scares me."
- Whirlpool: Unknown voice: "Someone is collecting my holodisks. Stop them." Ada:
  "Your holodisks? They're Astra's!"

Outposts handing over the holodisk, said by whichever outpost Ada is talking to:
"We found this drifting near our outposts. Take it, with our thanks."

Boarding after a new galaxy unlocks, once, instead of "Welcome back, Navigator.":
Astra: "Welcome back, Navigator. A new galaxy is ready." Not shown when the
unlocked galaxy's story is still in development.

Hub holodisk purchase confirm: "Buy Holodisk?" with the shared shop strings.

## Chapter one: the garden

Panel title: The Outposts.

Opening: "Ada has gardened with her grandmother since she was little. Today, her
grandmother mentions a nearby community garden that never got started. Its
organizer needs help, but nobody can look after everything."

Choices:

- "What kind of help do they need?"
- "I'd like to meet the organizer."

Branch pages, then the story rejoins:

- "What kind of help do they need?": "Grandma smiles. 'Planting, watering, tools...
  more than one person can manage.'"
- "I'd like to meet the organizer.": "'They'd love that,' Grandma says. 'Most
  people only offer help when someone asks them.'"

Space dialogue, in story order:

Grower: "A visitor! We grow food here, but our equipment needs repairs. There's a
workshop out here somewhere. Would you take them some food and ask for help?"
Ask more: "We could grow more with help. We haven't spoken much with the other
outposts."

Workshop: "We can repair their equipment. We're short of parts, though. The
salvagers might have what we need. Take them this tool kit."
Ask more: "We repair tools, ships and growing equipment. Spare parts make a
difference!"

Salvager: "Useful tools! We have parts to spare, but our food is running low. You
know some growers? Take these parts to the workshop. We'll contact the growers."
Ask more: "We recover things people have lost or left behind. Plenty can be used
again."

Workshop, delivery complete: "Now we can repair the growers' equipment. We'll
arrange the next exchange ourselves. Thank you for introducing us!"

Home resolution: "Back home, Ada asks which jobs need doing. The organizer lists
planting, finding tools, watering and teaching new gardeners. Her grandmother
offers to help people learn."

Choices:

- "Ask what each person can offer."
- "Divide the work into smaller jobs."

Branch pages, then the story rejoins:

- "Ask what each person can offer.": "A neighbor can water on weekends. Another has
  spare tools. Grandma will teach. Nobody can do everything, but everyone can do
  something."
- "Divide the work into smaller jobs.": "Ada writes each job on a card: plant, water,
  fetch tools, teach. Small jobs are easier to say yes to, and soon every card has
  a name."

Closing: "On planting day, neighbors bring tools, plants and time. They don't all
have to do the same thing. The garden has begun."

Objectives, in order:

1. Explore the galaxy.
2. Take food from the growers to the workshop.
3. Take the workshop's tool kit to the salvagers.
4. Bring spare parts from the salvagers to the workshop.
5. Collect the holodisk from the outposts.
6. Return home when ready.

## Chapter two: the mining hub

Panel title: The Mining Hub.

Opening: "At school, Ada sees a child being pushed out of a playground game. Other
children look away. There is no excuse for treating someone that way. Ada
wonders who else noticed."

Choices:

- "Notice who might help."
- "Think about how to offer support."

Branch pages, then the story rejoins:

- "Notice who might help.": "A few children look uncomfortable, not amused. Ada
  guesses they don't like it either. They just don't know what to do yet."
- "Think about how to offer support.": "Ada thinks about what she would want:
  someone beside her, and an invitation to join in. No speeches, just company."

Space dialogue, in story order:

Station, on arrival and on deposits between milestones: "Welcome. Shoot resource
rocks and collect what they release. Bring cargo here to deposit it. Partial
loads count, and you can travel back when you're out of danger."
Ask more: "Independent miners bring resources here for transport. There is
docking space for everyone."

Ada, first deposit milestone: "That pilot cut across the miner's approach and made
them retreat. There are free docks. Why won't they let that miner through?"

Miner, after the second deposit milestone: "It keeps happening. I've started
waiting until they're gone. Would I like company next time? Yes. That would
help."
Ask more: "I know how to dock. I just need them to stop blocking me."

Regular visitor: "I've seen it. I didn't know what to do. I'll come in with you.
We can make room and stay nearby."

Regular visitor, final deposit milestone: "There's room for everyone here. Let
them dock. We are staying. The miner approaches with company, docks and unloads.
'Thank you for staying. See you next trip.'"

Home resolution: "Back at school, Ada speaks privately with the child: 'I saw what
happened. Would you like to join us?' They would. Ada talks with her friends
about making room and refusing to laugh along."

Choices:

- "Make room in the game."
- "Sit together and invite friends over."

Branch pages, then the story rejoins:

- "Make room in the game.": "Ada's friends shuffle over and make a gap. The child
  steps in. When someone laughs, nobody laughs along."
- "Sit together and invite friends over.": "At lunch, Ada and the child sit
  together. One by one, Ada's friends join them, and the table gets louder and
  happier."

Closing: "Ada and her friends include the child. When the behavior happens again,
they stay supportive instead of leaving them alone."

Objectives, in order:

1. Explore the galaxy.
2. Mine resource rocks and deposit cargo at the hub.
3. Keep depositing resources and talk with the miner.
4. Speak with regular visitors about approaching together.
5. Join the miner's approach at the hub.
6. Buy the holodisk at the hub.
7. Return home when ready.

## Chapter three: the whale

Panel title: The Whale Family.

Opening: "Penny waits by the door. It's time for a walk, but Ada would rather keep
playing. 'Nobody has to take me for a walk,' she complains. She loves Penny, but
hasn't considered how much Penny depends on her."

Choices:

- "Wonder why walks matter so much."
- "Think about what Penny can choose."

Branch pages, then the story rejoins:

- "Wonder why walks matter so much.": "Penny can't open the door, read a map or
  choose a path. Every walk is her chance to explore."
- "Think about what Penny can choose.": "Ada can go anywhere she likes, even other
  galaxies. Penny can only go where Ada takes her."

Space dialogue, in story order:

Whale: "Can you hear me? I can't get through these rocks. Yes, I'm a whale, and I
can talk. Could you help clear a way?"
Ask more: "We travel through space together. There are places we visit and
currents we follow."

Ada, talking to the whale before the debris is cleared: "A whale in space! I'll
clear those rocks. My shots won't hurt you."

Whale, after the debris is cleared: "That's better. Thank you. But I don't know
where my family went. Would you look for them? They'll be searching for me too."
Ask more: "The young ones learn our routes from us. I like familiar voices and
finding places I haven't seen before."

Whale family: "Have you seen someone traveling alone? We've been searching.
They're safe? Please share our coordinates so they can find us."

Whale, on return: "You found my family! Now I know where to go. Thank you for
listening. The whale departs, and soon the family is traveling together again."

Home resolution: "Ada returns to the same moment. Penny is still waiting. She
remembers the whale's wish to move, explore and find its family. Penny needs
opportunities to experience the world too."

Choices:

- "Get the lead."
- "Let's go exploring."

Branch pages, then the story rejoins:

- "Get the lead.": "Penny's tail goes wild as Ada clips on the lead. 'Come on,
  girl. Let's see what's out there today.'"
- "Let's go exploring.": "Ada lets Penny choose the way. They take a new path
  through the park, and Penny sniffs every exciting thing."

Closing: "Ada takes Penny out and gives her time to stop, sniff and explore.
Caring means making time for what Penny needs."

Objectives, in order:

1. Explore the galaxy.
2. Shoot the debris blocking the whale.
3. Talk with the freed whale.
4. Explore to find the whale's family.
5. Return to the whale and share the coordinates.
6. Fight your way to the holodisk.
7. Return home when ready.

## In-flight conversations

Each speaker line wraps to at most two lines. First flight lessons, once each, skipped if already
done:

- Astra: "Use the left stick to fly." (desktop: "Use WASD to fly.")
- Astra: "Use the right stick to aim and fire." (desktop: "Use the arrow keys to
  aim and fire.")
- Astra: "That's Stardust. Fly close and I'll pull it in." Ada: "Like a magnet!"
- Astra: "A station! Fly into the dock to trade." Ada: "Shopping in space!"

Warnings:

- Shield low, once per fight: Astra: "Shield low!"
- Drones arrive: Astra: "Drones ahead." Ada: "I see them."

Penny senses a point of interest: Penny: "Woof! Woof!" Ada: "Penny, did you sense
something, girl?"

Reactions:

- New destination or station mapped: Astra: "New place mapped." Ada: "Our map is
  growing!"
- Capital ship sighted: Ada: "That ship is huge!" Astra: "No crew aboard. Aim for
  the glowing parts."

Sunflower:

- First arrival: Astra: "Sunflower. It's the only place I remember." Ada: "Then
  let's make new memories."
- Meeting the growers: Ada: "Who grows food out here?" Astra: "The growers farm
  starlight on their outpost."
- Meeting the workshop: Astra: "The workshop can fix almost anything." Ada:
  "Maybe they can fix you
  too, Astra!"
- Meeting the salvagers: Ada: "What do salvagers do?" Astra: "They find lost
  things and use them again."
- Quiet stretch: Ada: "Astra, do you ever get lonely?" Astra: "Not with a
  navigator aboard."
- Quiet stretch: Astra: "Penny's tail is wagging at my console lights." Ada: "She
  thinks you're fun."
- Quiet stretch: Ada: "Why is it called Sunflower?" Astra: "Its arms spiral like
  the seeds of a sunflower."

## Shared interface text

- Start screen: Ada the Navigator; Start on first launch, then Continue and
  Restart.
- Restart card: Restart? with Restart and Cancel.
- Galaxy selection Home button: Home.
- Home with nothing new: Narration: "Penny thumps her tail. Time for another
  adventure?" then Pick up the rock.
- Actions: Talk, Deliver, Deposit, Continue, Ask more, Use.
- Status labels: Objective, Nearby discovery, Shield, Cargo, Stardust.
- Pause: Pause (button label), Paused, Resume, Travel, Settings, Return home.
- Storybook pages: Previous, Next, Skip. Boarding from Ada's room: Pick up the rock.
- Galaxy selection: Galaxy {number}, Previous, Jump, Next. A locked galaxy's name and
  text: ...
- Galaxy names and flavor text:
  - Galaxy 1, Sunflower: "Scattered outposts grow, build and salvage, each on
    their own."
  - Galaxy 2, Cartwheel: "A busy mining hub where every rock is worth something."
  - Galaxy 3, Whirlpool: "Swirling debris, and a song somewhere in the dark."
  - Galaxy 4, Black Eye: "A shadowed rim where someone is watching."
- Travel card: Discovered destinations. With none yet: No destinations mapped yet.
- Unavailable in danger: Unavailable while you are in danger.
- Shield depletion: Astra jumped you home to recharge. Your story progress is
  saved.
- After the sleep scene, morning: Narration: "Astra recharges overnight. In the
  morning, the rock is warm again."
- Station welcome: Welcome aboard. Trade your Stardust for weapons and ship
  upgrades.
- Saving failure: Couldn't save progress. You can keep playing, but recent
  progress may be lost if you close the game.
- Completed story: Chapter complete. You can keep exploring.
- Galaxy four: story in development

Galaxy four has no playable story or invented finale in this prototype. Keep its
unfinished status explicit in development builds.

## Install steps

Shown when the browser holds no install prompt. Title, then steps:

- iOS: "Add to Home Screen" / "Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen."
- Firefox for Android: "Add app to Home screen" / "Tap the menu, then More, then
  Add app to Home screen."
- Chrome or Edge for Android: "Install app" / "Tap the menu, then Add to Home
  screen or Install app."
- Samsung Internet: "Add to Home screen" / "Tap the menu, then Add page to, then
  Home screen."
- Chrome or Edge on a computer: "Install app" / "Choose Install in the address bar or
  the browser menu."
- Safari on a Mac: "Add to Dock" / "Choose File, then Add to Dock."
- Firefox on a computer: "Not available in Firefox" / "Firefox can't install this game.
  Open it in Chrome or Edge to install."
- Any other browser: "Install" / "Use your browser's menu to install it or
  add it to your home screen."

## Landing page

- Pitch: Explore space. Bring new ideas home.
- Subline and meta description: Join Ada on space adventures and help her community
  back home with what she learns.
- Mobile: Move with the left stick. Aim and fire with the right stick. Tap Use to
  interact.
- Desktop: Move with WASD. Aim and fire with the arrow keys. Press Space to
  interact and Escape to pause.
