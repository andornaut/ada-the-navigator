# Ada the Navigator design

[The prototype contract](prototype.md) defines the current version one stories,
mechanics, presentation and persistence rules. [Ideas](ideas.md) contains proposals
outside the current version. These documents describe intended behavior; they do
not claim that features are implemented.

Ada is a curious, intelligent and kind 12-year-old girl living in Toronto with
her mom, dad, two cats and a dog. She enjoys finding, digging up and polishing
rocks. A special rock reveals itself as a personal spaceship when held; its rock
form remains provisional.

Every journey returns Ada to the same Earth moment at which she left. Observers
see no absence. She does not age through her travels but retains her experiences
and knowledge, which help her respond to local problems. Whether physical things
can return to Earth remains unspecified.

Toronto's interactive story and procedural space adventures continue together
throughout the game. The first three chapters concern a community garden,
playground bullying and caring for her dog. A fourth concluding chapter is within
version one but remains undesigned.
