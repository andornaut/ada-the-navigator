# Cheats

Cheats are available through settings in browser tabs only. Use the published
`runsInAppWindow` check both when opening cheats and when executing an action.
Installed PWA windows must reject both operations.

Group actions under **Save-changing** and **Run-only**. The approved action is
**Grant all weapons**, in Save-changing. No run-only action is specified.

Grant all weapons idempotently adds rapid fire, spread and piercing to the current
flight's owned weapons and persists through the normal save path. Selection, shield,
story, cargo and fire cooldown remain unchanged. This grants no permanent unlock;
normal shield-depletion and galaxy-departure rules still apply.

Without an active flight, disable the grant action. It must not launch a flight.
