Ada the Navigator

Explore space. Bring new ideas home.

Join Ada on space adventures and help her community back home with what she learns.

Play →
  • Mobile

    Move with the left stick. Aim and fire with the right stick. Tap Use to interact.

  • Desktop

    Move with WASD. Aim and fire with the arrow keys. Press Space to interact and Escape to pause.
