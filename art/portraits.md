# Portraits

Approved portraits reused from the Archie repository (`andornaut/archies-dog-walks`) at exact commit `8124ae5a9281e67b51191d8a9a609fc7f76480af`.

Copyright (c) 2026 andornaut. Source portraits are licensed under
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
The [source licence](portraits-LICENSE.txt) is included unchanged. PNGs are
unchanged; JSON metadata uses local image filenames and normalized formatting.

Ada is the brown/dark-skinned girl depicted in the source art. Penny is the golden dog portrait (`face-dog-penny`).

The `casey`, `trista` and `mr-b` assets are reusable community portraits from Archie. `trista` is Ada's mom and `mr-b` is her dad, Bin; `casey` assigns no role. These filenames and preserved source frame keys identify asset provenance. Tech selects the frame for each character. Each portrait includes a neutral expression at frame 1.

## Geometry and tags

Every frame is 32 × 32 pixels, untrimmed, with a 1000 ms duration. The Ada, casey, trista and mr-b sheets are 64 × 32 pixels; the Penny sheet is 96 × 32 pixels.

| Portrait | happy   | neutral | upset       |
| -------- | ------- | ------- | ----------- |
| Ada      | Frame 0 | Frame 1 | Not present |
| casey    | Frame 0 | Frame 1 | Not present |
| mr-b     | Frame 0 | Frame 1 | Not present |
| Penny    | Frame 0 | Frame 1 | Frame 2     |
| trista   | Frame 0 | Frame 1 | Not present |

PNG files are byte-for-byte copies; the manifest names their frames. Ada's and Penny's Aseprite data is kept in `art/sources/portraits/` for the compact crops, not shipped, and changes only `meta.image` to the destination PNG filename. Source frame keys, geometry, durations and tags are preserved.

`ada-compact.png` and `penny-compact.png` are adaptations under the same
licence: exact 22 × 22 crops of each frame, generated by
`art/tools/portraits.ts` with the source tags and durations. The Astra,
Admiral Archie and Grandma sheets and the cast portraits, Phantom's Blurb
portraits ([Phantom](phantom.md#portraits)) among them, are authored for Ada.
All are described in [Conversation portraits](conversation.md).

Needle's Avi portraits, Elder Loom's among them, are authored for Ada; see
[Elder Loom's portrait](needle.md#elder-looms-portrait) and
[Avi portraits](needle.md#avi-portraits).

## Storybook sprite source

`art/sources/face-walker-ada.png` is a byte-identical copy of the Archie source
`public/art/sprites/face-walker-ada.png` at the pinned commit above (SHA-256
`46633962fb8d3d4757954009b614a940d0e918dccb90e350b4b7e60d2f9deaa3`, the same
bytes as `public/art/portraits/ada.png`), under the same licence.
`art/tools/ada-sprites.ts` reads its neutral frame for the head of Ada's
storybook sprites ([storybook](storybook.md#adas-sprites)) and adapts it there;
the portrait files above stay unchanged.

`art/sources/face-walker-trista.png` (SHA-256
`bf8f6344b56ee5bcca4f66bfa01c188e4d3a8481a3f87b385936f4f1d8b361d0`) and
`art/sources/face-walker-mr-b.png` (SHA-256
`3f2d78aac50e5dac51077a9069c85bf95a0bb6705437d8528098401b9b94f51c`) are
byte-identical copies of the Archie sources `public/art/sprites/face-walker-trista.png`
and `public/art/sprites/face-walker-mr-b.png` at the pinned commit above (the
same bytes as `public/art/portraits/trista.png` and `mr-b.png`), under the same
licence. `art/tools/family-sprites.ts` reads their neutral frames, unchanged,
for the heads of Mom's and Dad's storybook sprites
([storybook](storybook.md#story-figures)); their outfit colours follow the
Archie walker sprites `walker-trista` and `walker-mr-b`.

## Admiral Archie source

`art/sources/admiral-archie-neutral.png` is the approved 32 px red-uniform
head-and-shoulder adaptation of Archie's dog, with the established officer
pose and cap. Its SHA-256 is
`cccdc6efeeeb954c57958eaf4856dc7c187ba0b0317abc0aa84f18275d6b70d1`.
The head and ruff derive from neutral frame 1 of
`public/art/sprites/face-dog-archie.png` in Archie at
`c7141cd8192289b9b42404ac2cf538023f486d0f` (source SHA-256
`20aaa3fa0ef7dacb3ee7902c8536c0f27ff9eb01a40bd646427eabb8a6a60463`),
under the same licence. `art/tools/core-busts.ts` copies this adapted neutral
frame for idle, animates the mouth for speaking, and uses its silhouette
for unknown frames. The full and compact contracts are in
[conversation portraits](conversation.md).
